Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in Tokyo & Shinjuku Skyline Evening Walk

Tokyo, Japan
Private Executive Chauffeur from Haneda/Narita Welcome Izakaya Dinner & Japanese Craft Beer The Capitol Hotel Tokyu / Cerulean Tower Tokyu Hotel

Touch down at Tokyo Haneda or Narita Airport. Your private driver transfers you into the heart of Tokyo. Check into your luxury high-rise hotel. As neon signs illuminate the metropolis, explore the buzzing alleys of Omoide Yokocho and view the Shinjuku skyline from above.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Haneda / Narita Airport Shinjuku Neon District Omoide Yokocho Lantern Alley Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building Lookout
02
Day 2

Historic Asakusa Senso-ji & Meiji Shinto Shrine

Tokyo
Tokyo Metro & Private Coach Breakfast, Tsukiji Outer Market Seafood Lunch, Dinner The Capitol Hotel Tokyu

Walk through the giant red Thunder Gate into Senso-ji, Tokyo’s oldest Buddhist temple founded in 645 AD. Browse Nakamise shopping street for traditional senbei rice crackers. Stroll through the tranquil cedar forest surrounding Meiji Jingu Shrine and watch the famous scramble at Shibuya Crossing.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Senso-ji Temple & Kaminarimon Nakamise Dori Crafts Tsukiji Outer Fish Market Meiji Jingu Shrine & Shibuya Crossing
03
Day 3

Mount Fuji 5th Station & Lake Kawaguchiko Cruise

Mount Fuji & Hakone Area
Private Panoramic Highway Coach & Lake Boat Breakfast, Hoto Noodle Lunch, Teppanyaki Dinner The Capitol Hotel Tokyu

Travel to the sacred volcanic cone of Mount Fuji. Ascend by road to the 5th Station at 2,305m above sea level for views above the clouds. Cruise across mirror-smooth Lake Kawaguchiko with Mount Fuji reflected on the water, and visit the iconic red Chureito Pagoda.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Mount Fuji 5th Station Lake Kawaguchiko Scenic Boat Chureito Pagoda & Fuji View Oshino Hakkai Spring Ponds
04
Day 4

Shinkansen to Alpine Takayama & Historic Sanmachi Suji

Tokyo to Takayama (Gifu Prefecture)
Tokaido Shinkansen & Hida Wide View Mountain Express Breakfast, Bento Lunch, Hida Beef Kaiseki Dinner Honoki no Sato Wanosato / Ryokan Tanabe Takayama

Board the Shinkansen bullet train traveling through mountain river valleys to the Edo-period merchant town of Takayama. Check into an authentic traditional Ryokan. Walk through the preserved dark-wood sake breweries and craft merchant houses of Sanmachi Suji, and soak in natural thermal onsen baths.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Nagoya Shinkansen Junction Hida Scenic Valley Rail Sanmachi Suji Historic Quarter Traditional Hot Spring Onsen Bath
05
Day 5

UNESCO Shirakawa-go Gassho Farmhouses & Transfer to Kyoto

Shirakawa-go & Kyoto
Mountain Coach & Shinkansen Bullet Train Breakfast, Alpine Soba Lunch, Kyoto Tofu Dinner The Thousand Kyoto / Hotel The Celestine Kyoto Gion

Visit the fairytale UNESCO village of Shirakawa-go, famous for its steep thatched-roof gassho-zukuri farmhouses built to withstand heavy mountain snows. Tour the inside of Wada House before taking the train south to Kyoto, the imperial capital of Japan for over a thousand years.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Shirakawa-go Ogimachi Village Shiroyama Viewpoint Wada House Gassho Farmhouse Kyoto Station Monument
06
Day 6

Kinkaku-ji Golden Pavilion & Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Kyoto
Private Coach & Romantic Sagano Railway Breakfast, Shojin Ryori Zen Lunch, Gion Dinner The Thousand Kyoto

Gaze in awe at Kinkaku-ji, the top two floors covered entirely in pure gold leaf reflecting in the Kyoko-chi mirror pond. Journey to Arashiyama to walk through the ethereal green canopy of the Sagano Bamboo Forest, visit the UNESCO garden of Tenryu-ji Temple, and cross the Togetsukyo Bridge.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) Arashiyama Bamboo Forest Tenryu-ji Zen Temple Garden Togetsukyo Moon Crossing Bridge
07
Day 7

Fushimi Inari 10,000 Torii Gates & Private Zen Tea Ceremony

Kyoto
Private Vehicle & Walking Breakfast, Kyoto Kaiseki Bento Lunch, Dinner The Thousand Kyoto

Walk the winding vermilion tunnels of Fushimi Inari Taisha, where 10,000 torii gates snake up sacred Mount Inari dedicated to the Shinto deity of rice and commerce. In the afternoon, enter a secluded temple sub-temple for a private Zen Tea Ceremony (chanoyu) with a master tea practitioner.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Fushimi Inari Shrine Torii Trails Okusha Inner Shrine Daitoku-ji Zen Sub-Temple Authentic Chanoyu Tea Ceremony
08
Day 8

Historic Gion Geisha District & Kiyomizu-dera Temple

Higashiyama & Gion, Kyoto
Walking Tour Breakfast, Higashiyama Teahouse Lunch, Dinner The Thousand Kyoto

Climb the historic cobblestone slopes of Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka lined with traditional wooden teahouses. Visit Kiyomizu-dera, perched on massive wooden pillars without a single nail. In the evening, walk the lantern-lit alleys of Gion along the Shirakawa canal, catching glimpses of Geikos and Maikos.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Kiyomizu-dera Wooden Stage Ninenzaka & Sannenzaka Lanes Yasaka Shrine Gion Hanamikoji Lantern Street
09
Day 9

Ancient Nara Deer Park & Todai-ji Giant Bronze Buddha

Nara & Kyoto
Kintetsu Express Train Breakfast, Nara Traditional Soba Lunch, Farewell Feast The Thousand Kyoto

Travel to Japan’s first permanent imperial capital, Nara. Bow to the friendly sacred shika deer roaming freely throughout Nara Park. Stand inside Todai-ji Temple, the world’s largest wooden building, housing the colossal 15-meter Daibutsu (Great Bronze Buddha), cast in 752 AD.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Nara Deer Park Todai-ji Great Buddha Hall (Daibutsu) Kasuga Taisha Lantern Shrine Farewell Wagyu Beef Sukiyaki Feast
10
Day 10

Haruka Express to Kansai Airport & Departure

Kyoto to Kansai International Airport (KIX)
JR Haruka Airport Express Train (First Class) Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy your final Japanese breakfast. Board the JR Haruka Express train directly from Kyoto Station to Kansai International Airport in Osaka (KIX) for your flight connection home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Kyoto Station JR Haruka Express Train Kansai International Airport (KIX)
