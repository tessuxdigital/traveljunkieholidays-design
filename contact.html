Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about booking, cancellations, visas, and private travel customization.

What is Travel Junkie Holidays's cancellation and refund policy?
Most Travel Junkie Holidays curated vacation packages allow 100% free cancellation up to 14 days before your scheduled departure date. For luxury hotel bookings, cancellation policies vary by hotel but are typically flexible up to 48 hours prior to check-in. All cancellation terms are transparently detailed on your confirmation voucher.
Are international and domestic flights included in packages?
Unless specifically stated otherwise in the package title, all our featured holiday packages include domestic flights or high-speed rail connections within the destination. International flights to/from your home airport can easily be bundled into your package at preferred airline partner rates.
How do I customize an existing package itinerary?
Simply submit the contact form above with your desired changes or call our 24/7 concierge at +1 (800) 555-8728. We can adjust trip duration, substitute hotels, add private helicopter excursions, or incorporate special celebration surprises at no extra planning fee.
Do you provide assistance with travel visas and travel insurance?
Yes! Travel Junkie Holidays coordinates with international consular agencies to provide comprehensive visa application guidance, official hotel invitation vouchers, and optional comprehensive travel medical and baggage insurance packages underwritten by premier global insurers.
