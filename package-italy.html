Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in Rome & Gelato Walk to the Trevi Fountain

Rome, Italy
Private Executive Chauffeur Welcome Roman Pasta Dinner Hotel de Russie / Grand Hotel de la Minerve Rome

Touch down at Rome Fiumicino Airport and enjoy a private transfer to your central family hotel near Piazza Navona. In the late afternoon, take a guided family gelato walk: toss a coin in the Trevi Fountain to ensure your return, admire the Pantheon, and sample artisan gelato.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Rome Fiumicino Airport Piazza Navona Trevi Fountain Pantheon Ancient Temple
02
Day 2

Colosseum Arena Floor & Roman Gladiator School

Rome
Private Van & Walking Breakfast, Trattoria Lunch, Dinner Hotel de Russie Rome

Enter the Colosseum through the Gladiator Gate directly onto the reconstructed wooden arena floor. Learn about chariot races at the Roman Forum. In the afternoon, head to the Appian Way for Roman Gladiator School, where kids suit up in tunics and learn ancient swordplay.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Colosseum Arena Floor Roman Forum & Palatine Hill Gladiator School Training Grounds Appian Way
03
Day 3

Vatican Museums, Sistine Chapel & St. Peter’s

Vatican City & Rome
Walking Tour Breakfast, Pizzeria Lunch, Dinner Hotel de Russie Rome

Skip the massive lines with a family-focused private Vatican guide. Discover the Map Gallery, Egyptian mummy relics, and gaze up at Michelangelo’s Sistine Chapel ceiling. Conclude at St. Peter’s Basilica and climb the dome for views across Rome.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Vatican Museums Sistine Chapel St. Peter’s Basilica & Square Castel Sant’Angelo
04
Day 4

Frecciarossa Train to Florence & Duomo Discovery

Rome to Florence, Italy
Frecciarossa 1000 High-Speed Train (1h 30m) Breakfast, Tuscan Trattoria Lunch, Gelato Stop Brunelleschi Hotel Florence

Board the ultra-sleek high-speed train gliding through the rolling green hills of Umbria and Tuscany to Florence. Check into your hotel steps from the Duomo. Walk across the medieval Ponte Vecchio bridge, marvel at Giotto’s Bell Tower, and rub the bronze snout of Il Porcellino for good luck.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Roma Termini Station Florence Santa Maria Novella Piazza del Duomo & Brunelleschi Dome Ponte Vecchio Bridge
05
Day 5

Michelangelo’s David & Family Pizza-Making Class

Florence
Walking Tour Breakfast, Hands-on Pizza Lunch & Dinner Brunelleschi Hotel Florence

Visit the Accademia Gallery to view Michelangelo’s towering masterpiece, the original David statue. In the afternoon, participate in a private family cooking academy where a master pizzaiolo teaches parents and kids how to stretch authentic dough, add fresh toppings, and bake pizzas in a roaring wood oven.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Accademia Gallery (David Statue) Piazza della Signoria Authentic Family Pizza Academy Piazzale Michelangelo Sunset
06
Day 6

High-Speed Rail to Venice & Private Gondola Ride

Florence to Venice, Italy
High-Speed Train & Private Wooden Water Taxi Breakfast, Venetian Seafood Lunch, Dinner Hotel Danieli / Baglioni Hotel Luna Venice

Travel by high-speed rail to Venice Santa Lucia Station. Step directly out of the station onto the Grand Canal, where your private polished mahogany water taxi transfers you to your lagoon hotel. In the late afternoon, step into an authentic black lacquer gondola for a relaxing cruise through peaceful canals.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Venice Santa Lucia Station Grand Canal Water Taxi Rialto Bridge Private Gondola Cruise
07
Day 7

St. Mark’s Basilica, Doge’s Palace & Murano Glass

Venice & Murano Island
Private Water Taxi Breakfast, Island Seafood Lunch, Farewell Dinner Hotel Danieli Venice

Tour the golden mosaics of St. Mark’s Basilica and cross the Bridge of Sighs into the secret dungeons of the Doge’s Palace. Board your private water taxi across the lagoon to Murano island for a private glassblowing masterclass where artisans shape molten glass into colorful horses and vases.

Featured Spots & Activities:
St. Mark’s Basilica Doge’s Palace & Bridge of Sighs Murano Island Glass Factory Burano Lace Island
08
Day 8

Venice Water Taxi Transfer & Departure

Venice Marco Polo Airport
Private Water Taxi to Airport Pier Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy your final Venetian espresso and breakfast overlooking the lagoon. Your private water taxi picks you up directly at the hotel jetty, cruising across the open waters to the airport docks of Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE) for your departure flight.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Hotel Private Water Dock Venice Lagoon Cruise Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE)
