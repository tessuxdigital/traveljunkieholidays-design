Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in Sydney & Circular Quay Harbour Walk

Sydney, Australia
Private Executive Chauffeur Harbour Welcome Dinner Shangri-La Sydney (Harbour View)

Arrive at Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport where your private driver welcomes you for a transfer to your harbour-facing hotel in The Rocks. Stroll around Circular Quay, taking in unobstructed views of the Sydney Opera House and the Harbour Bridge.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sydney Airport The Rocks Historic Quarter Circular Quay Sydney Harbour Bridge
02
Day 2

Sydney Opera House Tour & Taronga Zoo Ferry

Sydney
Harbour Catamaran Ferry & Walking Breakfast, Waterfront Café Lunch Shangri-La Sydney

Take an insider walking tour through the soaring sails and concert halls of the Sydney Opera House. Hop on a quick harbour ferry across to Taronga Zoo, where you will ride the sky safari cable car and meet friendly koalas, kangaroos, wallabies, and platypuses.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sydney Opera House Inside Tour Taronga Zoo Wildlife Sanctuary Sky Safari Cable Car Bradleys Head Coastal Walk
03
Day 3

Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk & Surfing Lesson

Bondi Beach, Sydney
Private Chauffeur Breakfast, Bondi Icebergs Lunch Shangri-La Sydney

Travel to world-famous Bondi Beach. Kids and parents can participate in a beginner surf lesson with licensed lifeguards, followed by lunch at Bondi Icebergs overlooking the ocean pool. Walk the cliffside path to Bronte and Coogee Beach.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Bondi Beach Surf School Bondi Icebergs Ocean Pool Bondi to Bronte Coastal Walk Tamarama Sculpture Lookout
04
Day 4

Blue Mountains & Scenic World Railway

Blue Mountains National Park, NSW
Private Luxury Coach Breakfast, Mountain View Lunch, Australian BBQ Dinner Shangri-La Sydney

Head inland to the UNESCO Blue Mountains. See the Three Sisters rock formation at Echo Point and ride the Scenic Railway—the world steepest passenger railway into the ancient Jamison Valley rainforest basin. Glide across the gorge on the glass-floored Scenic Skyway.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Echo Point & Three Sisters Scenic World Railway & Cableway Jamison Valley Rainforest Boardwalk Wentworth Falls Lookout
05
Day 5

Flight to Tropical Cairns & Palm Cove Check-In

Sydney to Cairns, Queensland
Domestic Flight (included) & Private Chauffeur Breakfast, Tropical Fruit Welcome, Seafood Dinner Pullman Palm Cove Sea Temple Resort

Fly north to tropical Cairns in Far North Queensland. Transfer to the relaxed seaside village of Palm Cove, where ancient paperbark trees line the beach. Spend the afternoon lounging around lagoon pools and watching tropical parrots.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sydney Domestic Airport Cairns Tropical Airport Palm Cove Esplanade Melaleuca Paperbark Boardwalk
06
Day 6

Outer Great Barrier Reef Marine Pontoon Day

Great Barrier Reef Marine Park
High-Speed Luxury Catamaran Breakfast, Tropical Smorgasbord Lunch Aboard Pullman Palm Cove Sea Temple Resort

Cruise aboard a high-speed catamaran to an outer reef activity platform on Moore Reef. Snorkel in crystal-clear water with sea turtles, clownfish, and neon corals. Non-swimmers and young kids can view the marine life from a semi-submersible submarine and underwater observatory.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Moore Reef Activity Platform Semi-Submersible Coral Tour Underwater Observatory Guided Marine Snorkel Tour
07
Day 7

Kuranda Scenic Railway & Skyrail Rainforest Cableway

Kuranda & Barron Gorge National Park
Historic Heritage Train & Aerial Gondola Breakfast, Rainforest Café Lunch Pullman Palm Cove Sea Temple Resort

Board the historic Kuranda Scenic Railway, winding through 15 hand-carved tunnels and past roaring Barron Falls. Explore Kuranda mountain heritage village, then glide over the canopy on the 7.5km Skyrail Rainforest Cableway.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Kuranda Scenic Railway Barron Falls Lookout Station Kuranda Heritage Markets Skyrail Rainforest Gondola
08
Day 8

Daintree Rainforest & Wildlife Crocodile River Cruise

Daintree National Park & Cape Tribulation
4WD Expedition Coach & River Boat Breakfast, Traditional Aussie Damper Lunch Pullman Palm Cove Sea Temple Resort

Journey into the oldest living tropical rainforest on earth: the Daintree. Cruise the Daintree River spotting wild saltwater crocodiles and rare birds. Walk the elevated boardwalks to Cape Tribulation where the rainforest directly meets the reef.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Daintree River Crocodile Cruise Cape Tribulation Beach Marrdja Botanical Boardwalk Mossman Gorge River Walk
09
Day 9

Cairns Lagoon Relaxation & Aboriginal Cultural Show

Cairns
Private Shuttle Breakfast, Tropical Lunch, Aboriginal Feast Dinner Pullman Palm Cove Sea Temple Resort

Spend a leisurely morning swimming in the saltwater lagoon on Cairns Esplanade. In the evening, attend a traditional Tjapukai Aboriginal cultural experience featuring boomerang throwing, didgeridoo music, and dreamtime storytelling around a ceremonial fire.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Cairns Esplanade Lagoon Rusty’s Farmers Market Aboriginal Cultural Performance Farewell Australian Seafood Feast
10
Day 10

Departure from Cairns

Cairns International Airport
Private Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy a final tropical fruit breakfast listening to birdsong in the paperbark trees. Check out of your resort, and meet your private chauffeur for the short drive to Cairns International Airport (CNS) for your flight home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Palm Cove Beachfront Resort Check-out Cairns International Airport (CNS)
