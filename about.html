A Desk That Speaks to College Departments

Travel Junkie Holidays plans industrial visits for colleges. Departments were spending weeks on plant emails, bus quotes, and hostel beds. We took that work into one itinerary a faculty in-charge can approve.

Today the same desk handles automobile, steel, pharma, electronics, and energy plants across India. Every quote lists the batch size, the per-student cost, the stay, and the documents the plant needs before the gate opens.

Our Mission

To take college batches onto real shop floors with permissions, transport, and a stay arranged before the students leave campus.

  • Eliminate travel friction through obsessive 24/7 concierge trip planning.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the world’s most trusted and visionary travel companion, leading the international tourism industry toward regenerative ecotourism where every journey enriches both the traveler and the visited host community.

  • Achieve complete carbon neutrality across 100% of our holiday packages.
  • Expand access to rare cultural preservation sanctuaries across all continents.
  • Set the global benchmark for seamless digital and human travel concierge care.
