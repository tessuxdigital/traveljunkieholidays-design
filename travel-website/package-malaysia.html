Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Kuala Lumpur Arrival & Petronas Twin Towers Skybridge

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Private Executive Chauffeur Transfer Welcome Dinner & Jalan Alor Hawker Tasting Mandarin Oriental Kuala Lumpur / Grand Hyatt KL (5-Star)

Land at Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) and meet your private chauffeur for the drive into the futuristic skyline of KL. Check into your hotel adjoining KLCC Park. Walk the double-decker Skybridge of the Petronas Twin Towers at level 41 and visit the 86th-floor observation deck. In the evening, sample grilled satay skewers on bustling Jalan Alor street.

Featured Spots & Activities:
KLIA International Airport Petronas Twin Towers Skybridge & Level 86 Deck KLCC Park Lake Symphony Fountains Jalan Alor Night Food Street
02
Day 2

Batu Caves Rainbow Steps & Historic Merdeka Square

Kuala Lumpur
Private Air-Conditioned Coach Breakfast, Nasi Lemak Lunch, Rooftop Cocktails Mandarin Oriental Kuala Lumpur

Climb the 272 vibrant rainbow-colored stone steps flanked by cheeky macaque monkeys into the Cathedral Cave of Batu Caves, guarded by the 42-meter golden statue of Lord Murugan. Return to downtown to visit Merdeka Square, the Moorish Sultan Abdul Samad Building, and the convergence of two rivers at Masjid Jamek.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Batu Caves Rainbow Staircase & Cathedral Cave Lord Murugan Golden Statue Merdeka Square & Royal Selangor Club Sultan Abdul Samad Building
03
Day 3

Scenic Transfer to Penang via Penang Bridge & Clan Jetties

Kuala Lumpur to George Town, Penang
Private Executive Highway Coach across 13.5km Penang Bridge Breakfast, Ipoh White Coffee Lunch, Penang Hawker Feast Eastern & Oriental Hotel Penang (Historic 5-Star Seafront)

Drive north through lush tropical hills, stopping in historic Ipoh for authentic white coffee and dim sum. Cross the engineering marvel of the Penang Bridge onto Penang Island. Check in to the legendary colonial seafront Eastern & Oriental (E&O) Hotel. Walk along the historic wooden stilt Clan Jetties of Weld Quay.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Ipoh Old Town Heritage Stop Penang Strait Bridge Eastern & Oriental Hotel Chew Jetty Floating Wooden Village
04
Day 4

George Town Street Art, Kek Lok Si Temple & Penang Hill

George Town & Air Itam, Penang
Private Vehicle & Funicular Railway Breakfast, Peranakan Nyonya Lunch, Waterfront Dinner Eastern & Oriental Hotel Penang

Take a trishaw or walking tour through UNESCO George Town photographing famous interactive street art murals like "Kids on a Bicycle." Visit the magnificent Kek Lok Si Temple, the largest Buddhist temple in Malaysia. Ride the Swiss funicular railway up Penang Hill for panoramic views of George Town and the strait.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Armenian Street Murals Cheong Fatt Tze Blue Mansion Kek Lok Si Pagoda & Kuan Yin Bronze Statue Penang Hill Funicular Summit
05
Day 5

Tropical Spice Garden & Penang Airport Departure

Teluk Bahang to Penang International Airport (PEN)
Private Airport Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Spend a fragrant morning exploring the landscaped living collections at the Tropical Spice Garden along the northern coast. Complete check-out at your hotel before your private driver transfers you to Penang International Airport (PEN) for your onward flight connection.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Batu Ferringhi Coastal Drive Tropical Spice Garden Walk Penang International Airport (PEN)
