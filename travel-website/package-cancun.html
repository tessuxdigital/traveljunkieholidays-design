Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Cancun Arrival & Oceanfront Resort Check-In

Riviera Maya, Quintana Roo, Mexico
Private Executive Suburban Chauffeur Gourmet All-Inclusive Dining & Margaritas Secrets Maroma Beach Riviera Cancun / Rosewood Mayakoba (5-Star)

Arrive at Cancun International Airport (CUN) and meet your private chauffeur for transfer to your AAA 5-Diamond all-inclusive oceanfront sanctuary on Maroma Beach, recognized among the world’s top beaches. Savor chilled craft cocktails on your private terrace overlooking turquoise waves.

Cancun International Airport (CUN) Maroma Beachfront Private Oceanfront Terrace Sunset Beach Palapa Lounge
02
Day 2

Isla Mujeres Luxury Catamaran Sail & Coral Reef Snorkel

Cancun Bay & Isla Mujeres
45ft Sailing Catamaran All-Inclusive Breakfast, Catamaran Lunch, French Bistro Dinner Secrets Maroma Beach Riviera Cancun

Board a catamaran sailing into the bright turquoise bay. Snorkel among the coral gardens of El Farito and the MUSA underwater sculpture museum. Dock at Isla Mujeres to explore colorful Playa Norte and rent golf carts to tour the Punta Sur cliffs where the sun first touches Mexico.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Cancun Marina Pier MUSA Underwater Sculpture Reef Playa Norte White Sands Punta Sur Sea Cliffs
03
Day 3

Chichen Itza Wonder of the World & Ik Kil Cenote

Yucatan Peninsula
Private Air-Conditioned Suburban Breakfast, Yucatecan Hacienda Buffet, Dinner Secrets Maroma Beach Riviera Cancun

Beat the tour buses with early VIP access to Chichen Itza. Stand before the monumental El Castillo pyramid, the Great Ball Court, and the Temple of the Warriors with an expert archaeologist. Cool off by swimming in the emerald waters of Cenote Ik Kil beneath hanging liana vines.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Chichen Itza Kukulkan Pyramid Great Ball Court & Observatory Cenote Ik Kil Sacred Sinkhole Historic Valladolid Colonial Square
04
Day 4

Tulum Clifftop Mayan Ruins & Akumal Sea Turtles

Tulum & Akumal, Riviera Maya
Private Vehicle All-Inclusive Breakfast, Tulum Beach Club Lunch, Dinner Secrets Maroma Beach Riviera Cancun

Visit the clifftop fortress of Tulum, the only Mayan city built directly on the Caribbean coast. Descend the wooden stairs to the beach below the ruins for a swim. In the afternoon, snorkel in the calm waters of Akumal Bay where wild green sea turtles feed peacefully on sea grasses.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Tulum Clifftop Archaeological Zone El Castillo Coastal View Akumal Bay Sea Turtle Sanctuary Tulum Hotel Zone Boutiques
05
Day 5

Rio Secreto Underground River & Subterranean Caves

Playa del Carmen Nature Reserve
Private Van Breakfast, Jungle Reserve Lunch, Steakhouse Dinner Secrets Maroma Beach Riviera Cancun

Suit up with a wetsuit and headlamp to hike and swim through Rio Secreto, a crystal subterranean cave system millions of years old. Marvel at thousands of delicate stalactites and stalagmites hanging over turquoise fresh water in an otherworldly underground cathedral.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Rio Secreto Nature Reserve Subterranean River Caverns Crystal Cave Cathedral 5th Avenue Playa del Carmen Walk
06
Day 6

Resort Beachfront Relaxation & Private Tequila Tasting

Maroma Beach
Resort Leisure All-Inclusive Unlimited Gourmet Dining & Premium Tequilas Secrets Maroma Beach Riviera Cancun

Enjoy a day of pure relaxation on Maroma Beach. Paddleboard on calm water, recline in an oceanfront cabana, and attend an exclusive private Tequila & Mezcal masterclass with the resort master tequilero, pairing artisanal añejos with dark Mexican chocolates.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Maroma Beach Cabanas Hydrotherapy Spa Circuit Private Master Tequilero Tasting Sunset Beachfront Candlelit Dinner
07
Day 7

Morning Caribbean Dip & Chauffeur Transfer to CUN

Cancun International Airport
Private Executive Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Take one last morning swim in the warm Caribbean Sea. Enjoy breakfast on your terrace before checking out. Your private chauffeur transfers you to Cancun International Airport (CUN) for your flight home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Beachfront Morning Stroll Resort Check-out Cancun International Airport (CUN)
