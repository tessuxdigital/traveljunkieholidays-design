Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Phuket Arrival & Kata Beachfront Resort Check-In

Phuket, Thailand
Private VIP Airport Chauffeur Welcome Coconut Drink & Royal Thai Dinner The Shore at Katathani / Katathani Phuket Beach Resort (5-Star)

Land at Phuket International Airport and be greeted with cold towels and fragrant jasmine garlands. Take a comfortable private transfer along the scenic coastline to Kata Noi Beach. Check in to your beachfront pool villa, and relax watching the sunset over the Andaman Sea.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Phuket International Airport Kata Noi Beachfront Private Ocean View Villa Karon Viewpoint Sunset
02
Day 2

Phang Nga Bay & James Bond Island Sea Canoeing

Phang Nga Bay National Park
Luxury Cruiser & Guided Sea Inflatable Canoes Breakfast, Thai Buffet Lunch Aboard, Dinner The Shore at Katathani

Cruise into Phang Nga Bay where vertical limestone monoliths soar out of calm green waters. Expert paddlers guide your sea canoe through dark sea caves into hidden tidal lagoons (hongs) open to the sky. Visit Koh Tapu, celebrated as James Bond Island in "The Man with the Golden Gun," and the floating stilt village of Koh Panyee.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Ao Phang Nga National Park James Bond Island (Koh Tapu) Koh Panyee Floating Village Hong Island Sea Caves
03
Day 3

Speedboat to Phi Phi Islands: Maya Bay & Pileh Lagoon

Phi Phi Don & Phi Phi Leh
Private Twin-Engine Speedboat Breakfast, Island Beach Lunch, Sunset Cocktails SAii Phi Phi Island Village (Beachfront Bungalow)

Speed across turquoise waters to Phi Phi Leh. Walk the powdery white sands of Maya Bay framed by 100-meter karst cliffs. Leap from the boat into the calm emerald natural swimming pool of Pileh Lagoon. Cruise past Viking Cave and snorkel among sea anemones and clownfish before checking in to your beachfront bungalow.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Maya Bay Nature Reserve Pileh Lagoon Emerald Waters Viking Cave Bird Nest Cliffs Loh Dalum Bay Sunset
04
Day 4

Phi Phi Viewpoint Hike & Bamboo Island Snorkeling

Phi Phi Archipelago
Longtail Boat & Walking Breakfast, Bamboo Island Picnic, Seafood BBQ Dinner SAii Phi Phi Island Village

Hike to the highest viewpoint on Phi Phi Don for a 360-degree panorama of the double-bay isthmus. In the afternoon, board a traditional wooden longtail boat to Bamboo Island (Koh Mai Phai), an uninhabited paradise encircled by untouched shallow coral reefs.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Phi Phi Don Viewpoint 2 Bamboo Island Coral Reef Loh Ba Gao Bay Beachfront Fire Dancing Show
05
Day 5

Krabi Railay Beach Karsts & Phra Nang Cave Beach

Krabi & Railay Peninsula
Speedboat Transfer & Longtail Breakfast, Clifftop Thai Lunch, Dinner Rayavadee Resort Krabi (Luxury Pavilion)

Cruise from Phi Phi to the isolated peninsula of Railay in Krabi, cut off from the mainland by massive limestone bluffs and accessible only by water. Explore Phra Nang Cave Beach, walk beneath jungle-fringed stalactites, and watch rock climbers scale the sheer limestone pillars.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Railay West Beach Phra Nang Princess Cave Beach Tonsai Rock Climbing Cliffs The Grotto Cave Restaurant
06
Day 6

Four Islands Sunset Tour & Bioluminescent Plankton

Krabi Islands (Poda, Chicken, Tub Islands)
Private Longtail Boat Breakfast, Island Beach Lunch, Farewell Dinner Rayavadee Resort Krabi

Explore the offshore Krabi islands: walk across the sandbar connecting Koh Mor and Tub Island at low tide, snorkel off Chicken Island, and swim at Koh Poda. After sunset, slip into dark water to swim among glowing blue bioluminescent plankton that light up with every stroke.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Tub & Mor Island Sandbar (Talay Waek) Chicken Island (Koh Kai) Koh Poda Beach Bioluminescent Plankton Night Swim
07
Day 7

Return to Phuket / Krabi Airport & Departure

Krabi or Phuket Airport
Private Boat & Executive Van Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy your final morning with breakfast in the coconut groves. Take a private speedboat transfer to the pier where your chauffeur connects you to Krabi Airport (KBV) or Phuket Airport (HKT) for your departure flight.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Rayavadee Coconut Groves Pier Boat Transfer Krabi International Airport (KBV)
