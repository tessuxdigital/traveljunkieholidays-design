Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in Imperial Cusco & Acclimatization

Cusco, Peru (3,400m)
Private Executive Chauffeur Welcome Coca Tea & Traditional Andean Dinner Belmond Hotel Monasterio / Palacio del Inka Cusco

Land at Alejandro Velasco Astete Airport in high-altitude Cusco. Meet your guide who presents you with traditional warm coca tea to assist with altitude acclimatization. Settle into a converted 16th-century colonial monastery hotel and take a gentle stroll around the Plaza de Armas.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Cusco Airport Plaza de Armas San Pedro Local Market Twelve-Angled Stone Wall
02
Day 2

Sacred Valley of the Incas & Ollantaytambo Fortress

Pisac & Ollantaytambo (2,792m)
Private Expedition Van Breakfast, Sacred Valley Buffet Lunch, Dinner Tambo del Inka Luxury Resort Urubamba

Descend into the fertile Sacred Valley. Explore the massive hillside ruins of Pisac and browse weaving stalls. Continue past terraced corn fields to the living Inca town of Ollantaytambo to climb the colossal stone temple fortress.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Pisac Ruins & Artisanal Market Urubamba River Valley Ollantaytambo Sun Temple Fortress Living Inca Streetways
03
Day 3

Inca Trail Day 1: Km 82 to Wayllabamba Camp

Km 82 to Wayllabamba (3,000m)
Foot Trek (12 km / 5-6 hours) Breakfast, Chef-Prepared Trail Lunch & Camp Dinner Deluxe Expedition Mountain Camp

Arrive at the official checkpoint at Km 82 (Piscacucho). Cross the suspension bridge over the Urubamba River to begin the trail. Trek through cactus scrub and past the ancient archaeological site of Patallacta before reaching the grassy mountain camp at Wayllabamba.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Km 82 Piscacucho Checkpoint Patallacta Archaeological Site Wayllabamba Village Camp
04
Day 4

Inca Trail Day 2: Conquering Dead Woman’s Pass (4,215m)

Warmiwañusqa Pass to Pacaymayo Camp
Foot Trek (11 km / 6-7 hours) Breakfast, High-Altitude Trail Lunch & Camp Dinner Pacaymayo Mountain Camp (3,600m)

The most demanding day of the trek. Climb ancient stone staircases through cloud forests and high paramo grasslands up to the summit of Dead Woman’s Pass (Warmiwañusqa) at 4,215 meters. Celebrate with views of snow-draped peaks, then descend to camp in the Pacaymayo Valley.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Llulluchapampa Forest Plateau Dead Woman’s Pass Summit (4,215m) Pacaymayo Mountain Valley Camp
05
Day 5

Inca Trail Day 3: Runkurakay, Sayacmarca & Wiñay Wayna

Pacaymayo to Wiñay Wayna Camp (2,680m)
Foot Trek (16 km / 8 hours) Breakfast, Trail Lunch & Camp Celebration Dinner Wiñay Wayna Camp

The most scenic stretch of the Inca Trail. Cross the Runkurakay pass, explore the clifftop citadel of Sayacmarca, pass through an original Inca rock tunnel carved into solid granite, and descend into the lush subtropical cloud forest to the cascading terraces of Wiñay Wayna ("Forever Young").

Featured Spots & Activities:
Runkurakay Circular Ruins Sayacmarca Clifftop Fortress Phuyupatamarca "Town Above the Clouds" Wiñay Wayna Terraces
06
Day 6

Inca Trail Day 4: Sun Gate (Inti Punku) & Machu Picchu

Inti Punku & Machu Picchu Citadel
Foot Trek (5 km) & Return Bus to Aguas Calientes Breakfast, Sanctuary Lodge Lunch, Gourmet Dinner Inkaterra Machu Picchu Pueblo Hotel (Luxury Eco-Resort)

Wake at 4:30 AM to reach Inti Punku (The Sun Gate) just as dawn breaks. Watch morning mist roll back to unveil Machu Picchu. Spend the morning on a comprehensive guided circuit through temples, astronomical observatories, and royal residences, then relax in natural thermal springs in Aguas Calientes.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Inti Punku (Sun Gate) Machu Picchu Lost Citadel Temple of the Sun & Intihuatana Stone Aguas Calientes Town
07
Day 7

Huayna Picchu Peak Climb & Vistadome Train to Cusco

Machu Picchu to Cusco
Vistadome Glass-Ceiling Train & Private Transfer Breakfast, Train Snacks, Novoandina Farewell Dinner Belmond Hotel Monasterio Cusco

Re-enter the sanctuary in the morning for the optional hike up Huayna Picchu or Machu Picchu Mountain for dizzying vertical views. In the afternoon, board the Vistadome panoramic train featuring live Andean folklore music and dancing as you trace the gorge back to Cusco.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Huayna Picchu Peak (2,720m) Vistadome Scenic Railway Urubamba River Canyon Cusco San Blas Artisan Quarter
08
Day 8

Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca) Alpine Day Excursion

Vinicunca (5,036m), Pitumarca
4WD Expedition Van & Mountain Trail Breakfast, Andean Lodge Lunch, Dinner Belmond Hotel Monasterio Cusco

Embark on an early morning journey into the high Andes to witness the geological marvel of Rainbow Mountain. Hike past herds of alpacas and vicuñas beneath glaciers to the 5,036-meter viewpoint overlooking mineral stripes of red, gold, lavender, and turquoise.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Pitumarca Village Ausangate Glacier Range Rainbow Mountain Vinicunca Summit (5,036m) Red Valley Panorama
09
Day 9

Farewell Cusco & Flight to Lima

Cusco to Lima Airport
Private Airport Chauffeur Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy breakfast in the courtyard of your hotel and do some last-minute shopping for baby alpaca textiles in the San Blas artisan quarter. Meet your driver for transfer to Cusco Airport for your flight to Lima and onwards home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Hotel Monasterio Courtyard San Blas Artisan Market Cusco Airport (CUZ)
