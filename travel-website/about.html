Our Story: Over a Decade of Unlocking the World's Wonders

Travel Junkie Holidays was established in 2012 in New York City when a small collective of mountaineers, cultural historians, and seasoned international explorers grew weary of rigid, one-size-fits-all travel packages. We envisioned a world where curious travelers could venture anywhere on Earth with absolute peace of mind, genuine local respect, and effortless luxury.

Today, Travel Junkie Holidays has empowered over 50,000 adventurers to scale the Swiss Alps, dive the Great Barrier Reef, uncover ancient Egyptian temples, and rest in handpicked overwater bungalows in the Maldives. Our worldwide team of destination specialists personally inspects every hotel, tests every itinerary, and negotiates exclusive rates so you can travel effortlessly.

Mission and Vision

Guided by sustainable values, local community empowerment, and a relentless pursuit of memorable travel excellence.

Our Mission

To inspire, simplify, and elevate international travel by creating handcrafted, ethically responsible journeys that connect curious travelers with authentic local cultures, pristine landscapes, and unforgettable life moments.

  • Prioritize 100% fair-wage partnerships with native guides and local drivers.
  • Eliminate travel friction through obsessive 24/7 concierge trip planning.
  • Deliver high-end value with no hidden costs or surprise surcharges.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the world’s most trusted and visionary travel companion, leading the international tourism industry toward regenerative ecotourism where every journey enriches both the traveler and the visited host community.

  • Achieve complete carbon neutrality across 100% of our holiday packages.
  • Expand access to rare cultural preservation sanctuaries across all continents.
  • Set the global benchmark for seamless digital and human travel concierge care.
