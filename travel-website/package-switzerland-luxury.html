Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Zurich Limousine Transfer to Badrutt’s Palace St. Moritz

Zurich to St. Moritz (Engadin Valley)
Private Chauffeur-Driven Mercedes Maybach Welcome Imperial Caviar & Champagne Reception Badrutt’s Palace Hotel St. Moritz (Palace Suite)

Land at Zurich VIP terminal and be welcomed by your private chauffeur. Drive in a Maybach limousine through the Julier Pass into the Engadin Valley to St. Moritz. Check in at the legendary Badrutt’s Palace Hotel where kings and celebrities have wintered for over a century.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Zurich VIP Airport Terminal Julier Mountain Pass St. Moritz Lake Promenade Badrutt’s Palace Grand Hall
02
Day 2

Helicopter Flight Over Bernina Massif & Glacier Landing

Piz Bernina & Morteratsch Glacier
Private Airbus H130 Helicopter Breakfast, Glacier Caviar & Champagne Picnic, Michelin Dinner Badrutt’s Palace Hotel St. Moritz

Board a private helicopter for a breathtaking flight sweeping past the granite spires of Piz Bernina and the cascading icefalls of the Morteratsch Glacier. Land directly onto a secluded high-altitude glacier plateau for a champagne tasting before flying back over St. Moritz Lake.

Featured Spots & Activities:
St. Moritz Heliport Piz Bernina Summit (4,049m) Morteratsch High Glacier Via Serlas Designer Boutiques
03
Day 3

Glacier Express Excellence Class Journey to Andermatt

St. Moritz to Andermatt
Glacier Express Excellence Class (World’s Most Luxurious Train) Breakfast, 5-Course Excellence Class Wine-Pairing Lunch The Chedi Andermatt (Deluxe Suite)

Board the Glacier Express in prestigious Excellence Class. Sink into leather reclining window seats as a dedicated concierge serves a 5-course lunch paired with rare Swiss vintages. Cross the Landwasser Viaduct and Oberalp Pass into Andermatt to check into the Asian-alpine masterpiece, The Chedi.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Landwasser Viaduct Rhine Gorge (Swiss Grand Canyon) Oberalp Pass (2,033m) The Chedi 2,400m² Spa & Hydrotherapy
04
Day 4

Alpine Pass Supercar Tour & Transfer to Zermatt

Andermatt to Zermatt
Ferrari/Porsche Supercar Drive & Private Limousine Breakfast, Furka Pass Clifftop Lunch, Valais Degustation The Omnia Zermatt / Mont Cervin Palace Suite

Experience driving a high-performance sports car across the hairpin switchbacks of the Furka Pass where James Bond was filmed. Meet your private electric limousine at Täsch for transfer into car-free Zermatt. Check in at The Omnia, perched high on a rock cliff directly facing the Matterhorn.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Furka Pass Switchbacks Rhône Glacier Ice Grotto Täsch VIP Private Terminal The Omnia Clifftop Glass Elevator
05
Day 5

VIP Gornergrat Carriage & Matterhorn Heli-Skiing / Flight

Zermatt & Matterhorn Peak
Private Heritage VIP Train Carriage & Helicopter Breakfast, 2-Star Michelin Lunch, Private Chalet Dinner The Omnia Zermatt

Travel in an exclusive private carriage on the Gornergrat Bahn with champagne and pastries. Soar by helicopter around the iconic North Face of the Matterhorn, skimming just meters from sheer hanging seracs and granite arêtes before an exclusive lunch at Chez Vrony.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Gornergrat Private VIP Salon Car Matterhorn North Face Fly-By Chez Vrony Sun Terrace Zermatt Vintage Wine Vault
06
Day 6

Matterhorn Glacier Ride Crystal Cabin & Holistic Spa

Matterhorn Glacier Paradise (3,883m)
Swarovski Crystal Glass-Floor Cable Car Breakfast, Alpine Lounge Lunch, Farewell Gala Dinner The Omnia Zermatt

Ascend to Europe’s highest cable car station inside a Swarovski crystal-encrusted glass-bottom cabin that turns transparent mid-flight. In the afternoon, indulge in bespoke alpine botanical massages and warm basalt stone hydrotherapy overlooking the Matterhorn.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Crystal Cable Car Cabin Matterhorn Summit Observation Deck The Omnia Thermal Baths Michelin Gala Dinner
07
Day 7

Limousine Transfer to Zurich Airport Departure

Zermatt to Zurich Airport
Private Executive Maybach Limousine Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast on your private suite terrace. Your private chauffeur escorts you via luxury vehicle to Zurich International Airport (ZRH) with VIP fast-track lounge access.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Zermatt Private Terminal Visp Expressway Zurich Airport VIP Lounge
