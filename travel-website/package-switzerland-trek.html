Swiss Alps Glacier & Summit Trek

Trek the legendary trails beneath Europe’s most iconic summits: the Eiger, Mönch, Jungfrau, and the pyramid of the Matterhorn. Traverse panoramic suspension bridges, cross ancient glaciers, ride mountain cogwheel trains, and paraglide above turquoise alpine lakes.

Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Zurich Arrival & Scenic Train to Interlaken

Zurich to Interlaken, Switzerland
Swiss Federal Railways (SBB) First-Class Panoramic Train Traditional Swiss Cheese Fondue Welcome Dinner Victoria-Jungfrau Grand Hotel & Spa / Hotel Interlaken

Arrive at Zurich Airport and board your comfortable First-Class train curving along the shores of Lake Lucerne and over the Brünig Pass into Interlaken, situated between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz. Gather for a welcome briefing and an authentic Gruyère and Vacherin cheese fondue.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Zurich Airport Railway Station Brünig Pass Mountain Rail Interlaken Ost & Town Center Lake Brienz Waterfront
02
Day 2

Lauterbrunnen 72 Waterfalls & Mürren Cliff Trail

Lauterbrunnen Valley & Mürren
Mountain Cogwheel Rail & Cable Car Breakfast, Mountain Chalet Rösti Lunch, Dinner Victoria-Jungfrau Grand Hotel & Spa

Descend into the glacial Lauterbrunnen Valley, the inspiration for Tolkien’s Rivendell. Walk beneath the 300-meter Staubbach Falls and ride inside the mountain elevator to the Trümmelbach glacial cave falls. Ascend by cable car to car-free Mürren village and hike along the cliff edge overlooking the valley.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Staubbach Falls Trümmelbach Inside-Mountain Falls Car-Free Village of Mürren Mountain View Trail to Gimmelwald
03
Day 3

Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe & Aletsch Glacier

Jungfraujoch (3,454m)
Eiger Express Tricable Gondola & Jungfrau Railway Breakfast, Glacier Restaurant Lunch, Dinner Victoria-Jungfrau Grand Hotel & Spa

Board the state-of-the-art Eiger Express gondola to Eigergletscher station, then transfer to the historic cogwheel train tunneling through the Eiger to Europe’s highest station at 3,454m. Step out onto the Sphinx Observatory terrace gazing across the 22km Aletsch Glacier, walk through the sculpted Ice Palace, and trek across the glacier snowpack to Mönchsjochhütte hut.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Eiger Express Gondola Jungfraujoch Sphinx Observatory 22km Aletsch Glacier Basin Mönchsjochhütte Snow Trail
04
Day 4

Tandem Paragliding & Eiger Trail Beneath the North Face

Grindelwald & Interlaken
Tandem Paraglider & Grindelwald First Gondola Breakfast, Alpine Picnic Lunch Victoria-Jungfrau Grand Hotel & Spa

Experience the rush of tandem paragliding from Beatenberg, soaring 800m above turquoise lakes with views of the snow-capped Bernese Oberland. In the afternoon, hike the celebrated Eiger Trail, a 6km path tracing the base of the imposing 1,800m Eiger North Face where legendary mountaineering history was written.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Beatenberg Paragliding Launch Höhematte Interlaken Landing Eiger Trail Hiking Route Grindelwald Glacier Gorge
05
Day 5

Scenic Train to Zermatt & Car-Free Alpine Village

Interlaken to Zermatt (Valais Alps)
First-Class Train via Lötschberg Mountain Route Breakfast, Valais Raclette Dinner Grand Hotel Zermatterhof / Mont Cervin Palace

Board the scenic Lötschberg mountain train into the canton of Valais, transferring onto the narrow-gauge Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn into car-free Zermatt. Stroll past historic sun-darkened larch-wood chalets in the old village with the magnificent Matterhorn looming directly above.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Lötschberg Scenic Railway Zermatt Old Village Chalets Matterhorn Museum Zermatlantis Kirchbrücke Sunset Viewpoint
06
Day 6

Gornergrat Cogwheel Train & Matterhorn Riffelsee Trek

Gornergrat (3,089m) & Zermatt
Historic Gornergrat Cogwheel Railway Breakfast, Mountain Chalet Lunch, Dinner Grand Hotel Zermatterhof

Ride Switzerland’s first electric cogwheel railway up to the summit of Gornergrat at 3,089m, surrounded by 29 four-thousand-meter peaks and the Gorner Glacier. Hike down to Lake Riffelsee to photograph the iconic reflection of the Matterhorn in the mirror-smooth alpine water.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Gornergrat Summit Station Gorner Glacier Panorama Lake Riffelsee Reflection Riffelalp Pine Forest Trail
07
Day 7

Matterhorn Glacier Paradise (3,883m) & Suspension Bridge

Zermatt Summit Station
Matterhorn Glacier Ride 3S Cableway Breakfast, Panoramic Summit Lunch, Farewell Dinner Grand Hotel Zermatterhof

Soar on the highest 3S cableway in the world to the summit of Matterhorn Glacier Paradise at 3,883 meters. Tour the glacial ice crystal palace carved 15 meters inside the ice sheet. In the afternoon, hike to the world’s longest pedestrian suspension bridge spanning high above the mountain valley.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Matterhorn Glacier Ride Highest Ice Palace in the World Zermatt Glacier Trail Charles Kuonen Suspension Bridge
08
Day 8

Scenic Train to Zurich & Departure

Zermatt to Zurich Airport
First-Class Swiss Rail Train Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Savor one last morning gaze at the sun hitting the summit peak of the Matterhorn over breakfast. Board the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn connecting to Zurich Airport (ZRH) for your return flight home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Zermatt Station Visp Mountain Rail Junction Zurich Airport (ZRH)
