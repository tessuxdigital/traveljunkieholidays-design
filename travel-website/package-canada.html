Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Calgary Arrival & Drive into Banff National Park

Calgary & Banff, Alberta, Canada
Family Premium SUV Chauffeur Transfer Welcome Mountain Lodge Dinner Fairmont Banff Springs / Rimrock Resort

Land at Calgary International Airport and meet your driver. Head west as prairie plains give way to the soaring granite wall of the Canadian Rockies. Check in to the iconic "Castle in the Rockies," Fairmont Banff Springs, and stroll the alpine village of Banff.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Calgary International Airport Trans-Canada Rockies Highway Banff Alpine Townsite Bow Falls & Surprise Corner
02
Day 2

Banff Gondola, Sulphur Mountain & Lake Minnewanka

Banff National Park
Private SUV / Coach Breakfast, Summit Bistro Lunch, Campfire S’mores Dinner Fairmont Banff Springs

Ascend Sulphur Mountain on the Banff Gondola to an altitude of 2,281 meters for 360-degree views of six mountain ranges. Stroll the summit boardwalk, then take a private cruise on glacial Lake Minnewanka with wildlife spotting for bighorn sheep along the shoreline.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Banff Gondola Summit Sulphur Mountain Cosmic Ray Station Lake Minnewanka Cruise Banff Upper Hot Springs
03
Day 3

Emerald Waters of Lake Louise & Moraine Lake

Lake Louise & Valley of the Ten Peaks
Private National Park Shuttle Breakfast, Lakeside Tea House Lunch Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise

Marvel at the unreal electric-blue water of Lake Louise. Rent red cedar canoes for the kids to paddle beneath Victoria Glacier. Continue to the Valley of the Ten Peaks at Moraine Lake, climbing the rockpile trail for one of the most photographed vistas on earth.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Lake Louise Shoreline Victoria Glacier Lookout Moraine Lake & Rockpile Trail Lake Agnes Tea House Hike
04
Day 4

Icefields Parkway & Athabasca Glacier Explorer

Columbia Icefield & Jasper National Park
Ice Explorer All-Terrain Mega Vehicle Breakfast, Glacier View Lunch, Mountain Lodge Dinner Fairmont Jasper Park Lodge

Drive the spectacular Icefields Parkway, rated one of the world’s top scenic drives with over 100 visible glaciers. Board the mammoth Terra Bus Ice Explorer to walk on the 300-meter thick Athabasca Glacier. Step onto the glass-floored Glacier Skywalk suspended 280 meters above Sunwapta Valley.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Icefields Parkway Highway 93 Athabasca Glacier Walk Columbia Icefield Skywalk Sunwapta Falls & Athabasca Falls
05
Day 5

Jasper Wildlife Safari & Maligne Canyon Boat Tour

Jasper National Park
Park Ranger Safari Van & Lake Cruiser Breakfast, Lakeside Picnic, Jasper Grill Dinner Fairmont Jasper Park Lodge

Join an early morning naturalist safari through the Athabasca Valley, searching for grizzly and black bears, moose, and herds of elk. Hike through the 50-meter deep limestone gorge of Maligne Canyon, followed by a boat cruise across Maligne Lake to world-renowned Spirit Island.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Maligne Canyon Suspension Bridges Maligne Lake Boat Cruise Spirit Island Iconic View Jasper Dark Sky Preserve Stargazing
06
Day 6

Pyramid Lake Biking, Canoeing & Hot Springs

Jasper
Mountain Bikes & Private SUV Breakfast, Lakefront BBQ Lunch, Farewell Lodge Dinner Fairmont Jasper Park Lodge

Rent family mountain bikes along the scenic trails around Pyramid Lake and Patricia Lake with Mount Edith Cavell towering in the distance. In the afternoon, unwind at Miette Hot Springs before gathering around the outdoor campfire at Jasper Park Lodge for s’mores.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Pyramid Lake Island Patricia Lake Trail Miette Natural Hot Springs Lodge Campfire Stargazing
07
Day 7

Scenic Return Drive to Calgary & Departure

Jasper to Calgary Airport
Private Executive SUV Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast by the lake before beginning the scenic return journey through the Bow Valley back to Calgary. Transfer directly to Calgary International Airport (YYC) for your departure flight.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Bow Valley Parkway Castle Mountain Lookout Calgary International Airport (YYC)
