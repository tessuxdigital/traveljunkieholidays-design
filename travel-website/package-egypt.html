Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in Cairo & View of the Giza Pyramids

Cairo, Egypt
Private Executive Chauffeur Transfer Welcome Egyptian Mezze Dinner Marriott Mena House Cairo (Pyramid View Room)

Land at Cairo International Airport (CAI) where our VIP airport coordinator assists through immigration and customs. Transfer to the historic Marriott Mena House hotel, situated in the shadow of the Great Pyramids. Sip mint tea while gazing across the garden pool at the pyramid apex.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Cairo International Airport Marriott Mena House Historic Estate Giza Plateau Night Skyline Pyramid View Garden Terrace
02
Day 2

Giza Pyramids, Great Sphinx & Grand Egyptian Museum

Giza & Cairo
Private Chauffeur & Camel Ride Breakfast, Egyptian Grill Lunch, Dinner Marriott Mena House Cairo

Spend the morning on the Giza Plateau with your private Egyptologist. Gaze up at the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the only surviving Wonder of the Ancient World. Ride camels across desert sands for panoramic views of all three pyramids, stand before the enigmatic Great Sphinx, and tour the new Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM).

Featured Spots & Activities:
Great Pyramid of Khufu (Cheops) Pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure Great Sphinx of Giza Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)
03
Day 3

Fly to Luxor, Board 5-Star Nile Cruise & Karnak Temple

Cairo to Luxor
Domestic Flight (included) & 5-Star Luxury Nile Cruiser Breakfast, Cruise Welcome Lunch, Captain’s Dinner The Oberoi Zahra / Sanctuary Sun Boat (Luxury Nile Cruiser)

Fly south to Luxor, the ancient capital of Thebes. Check in to your riverfront stateroom aboard your 5-star Nile cruise ship. In the afternoon, tour the monumental Karnak Temple complex, walking through the Great Hypostyle Hall containing 134 towering sandstone columns. At dusk, visit illuminated Luxor Temple.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Luxor Airport 5-Star Nile Cruise Check-In Karnak Temple Complex & Hypostyle Hall Illuminated Luxor Temple
04
Day 4

Valley of the Kings, King Tut Tomb & Hatshepsut Temple

West Bank of Luxor
Air-Conditioned Coach & River Boat Breakfast, Cruise Lunch, Evening Galabeya Party Dinner 5-Star Luxury Nile Cruiser

Cross the Nile to the West Bank. Descend into royal painted tombs in the Valley of the Kings, including the burial chamber of King Tutankhamun. Visit the terraced mortuary temple of Queen Hatshepsut rising out of desert cliffs at Deir el-Bahari and the twin Colossi of Memnon.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Valley of the Kings (KV62 King Tut) Hatshepsut Terraced Temple Colossi of Memnon Sailing past Esna Lock
05
Day 5

Falcon God Temple of Edfu & Crocodile Temple of Kom Ombo

Edfu & Kom Ombo, Upper Egypt
Nile Cruise Ship & Horse Carriage in Edfu Breakfast, Sundeck Buffet Lunch, Nubian Dinner 5-Star Luxury Nile Cruiser

Take a horse carriage to the Temple of Horus at Edfu, the best-preserved cult temple in all of Egypt. Sail onward as date palms and golden sand dunes glide past the sundeck pool. In the afternoon, dock at Kom Ombo to explore the rare dual temple dedicated to Sobek the crocodile god and Horus the falcon.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Temple of Horus at Edfu Nile River Sundeck Cruising Kom Ombo Dual Temple Mummified Crocodile Museum
06
Day 6

Aswan High Dam, Philae Temple of Isis & Felucca Sail

Aswan, Egypt
Nile Cruiser, Motorboat to Island, & Traditional Felucca Breakfast, Island Lunch, Farewell Cruise Dinner 5-Star Luxury Nile Cruiser

Arrive in picturesque Aswan where desert dunes plunge directly into the Nile. Visit the modern engineering marvel of the Aswan High Dam. Take a boat to the romantic island temple of Philae dedicated to the goddess Isis. In the late afternoon, enjoy a peaceful sail on a traditional wooden felucca around Elephantine Island.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Aswan High Dam Philae Island Temple of Isis Unfinished Obelisk Granite Quarry Traditional Felucca Sail around Elephantine Island
07
Day 7

Abu Simbel Colossal Temples & Return Flight to Cairo

Abu Simbel & Cairo
Air-Conditioned Coach / Flight to Abu Simbel & Flight to Cairo Breakfast, Aswan Lunch, Cairo Gourmet Dinner Four Seasons Hotel Cairo at Nile Plaza

Travel south through the Nubian desert to the awe-inspiring rock-cut temples of Abu Simbel on Lake Nasser. Marvel at the four colossal 20-meter statues of Ramses II carved into the mountainside. Fly back to Cairo and check in at the Four Seasons on the banks of the Nile.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Abu Simbel Great Temple of Ramses II Temple of Queen Nefertari Lake Nasser Shoreline Four Seasons Nile Plaza Cairo
08
Day 8

Islamic & Coptic Cairo & Khan el-Khalili Bazaar

Old Cairo
Private Chauffeur Tour Breakfast, Historic Naguib Mahfouz Café Lunch, Dinner Four Seasons Hotel Cairo at Nile Plaza

Discover Coptic Cairo, visiting the Hanging Church and the Church of St. Sergius where the Holy Family took refuge. Ascend to the medieval Citadel of Saladin and the Mosque of Muhammad Ali. Conclude in the 14th-century labyrinth of Khan el-Khalili bazaar for brass lamps, spices, and perfumes.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Coptic Hanging Church Citadel of Saladin & Alabaster Mosque Khan el-Khalili Historic Bazaar El Fishawy Historic Teahouse
09
Day 9

Farewell Cairo & Airport Transfer Departure

Cairo International Airport
Private Executive Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy breakfast overlooking the Nile. Complete check-out before your private chauffeur transfers you to Cairo International Airport (CAI) with VIP airport lounge access for your flight home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Nile Promenade Morning Hotel Check-out Cairo International Airport (CAI)
