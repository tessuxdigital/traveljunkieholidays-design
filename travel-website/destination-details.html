Destination Overview

Nestled harmoniously between the crystal-clear azure waters of Lake Thun and Lake Brienz, Interlaken serves as the gateway to Switzerland's most legendary alpine peaks: the soaring trio of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau. Here, sheer vertical cliffs plunge into flower-carpeted meadows, glacial streams roar through quiet pine forests, and cogwheel trains have climbed near-vertical mountain slopes since the 19th century.

Whether you are an adrenaline seeker yearning to paraglide over dramatic glaciers, an avid hiker traversing the flower-filled Lauterbrunnen valley, or a luxury traveler savoring authentic cheese fondue aboard a historic paddle steamer, Switzerland delivers an unmatched harmony of pristine nature, flawless efficiency, and timeless European elegance.

Top Tourist Attractions

Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe

Europe’s highest railway station at 3,454m. Features the Ice Palace and breathtaking views over the 22km Aletsch Glacier.

Open Year-Round

The Matterhorn & Zermatt

The world’s most iconic pyramid-shaped mountain. Explore car-free Zermatt village with luxury horse-drawn carriages.

Gornergrat Railway

Lauterbrunnen 72 Waterfalls

The dramatic valley that inspired Tolkien’s Rivendell, featuring Staubbach Falls plunging 300 meters into the valley basin.

Trümmelbach Inside-Cave Falls

Lake Brienz & Harder Kulm

Turquoise waters framed by steep limestone cliffs, with the Harder Kulm funicular glass skybridge floating 1,322 meters above.

Historic Steamboat Cruises

Things to Do & Activities

Glacier Express Rail

Ride the "world’s slowest express train" across 291 bridges and 91 tunnels with panoramic glass dome roofs.

Tandem Paragliding

Launch from Beatenberg mountain and glide 800 meters over turquoise lakes with a certified Swiss pilot.

Glacier Skiing

Carve 360 kilometers of groomed ski runs beneath the Matterhorn, with year-round skiing on the Theodul Glacier.

Local Cuisine & Dining

Authentic Cheese Fondue

Simmered Gruyère & Vacherin Fribourgeois with garlic and white wine, served in a caquelon with crusty bread cubes.

Bernese Alpine Rösti

Golden pan-fried grated potatoes crisped in clarified alpine butter, often topped with fried eggs and melted cheese.

Artisan Chocolates & Truffles

Silky handcrafted pralines made with pure Alpine milk from generational chocolatiers in Interlaken and Zurich.

Best Time to Visit

Summer (June – August) · Peak Season Ideal for Hiking

Pleasant temperatures between 18°C–26°C. High mountain trails and pass roads are fully open, wildflower meadows blossom, and paddleboarding on the lakes is in full swing.

Winter (December – March) · Ski Season Ideal for Snow Sports

Snow blankets the valleys and peaks. World-class skiing, sledding, cozy fondue nights by wood fireplaces, and festive Christmas markets in historic town squares.

Spring & Autumn (April–May & Sep–Oct) · Shoulder Seasons Fewer Crowds

Lighter crowds, crisp mountain air, golden larch forests in autumn, and dramatic rushing waterfalls fueled by melting spring snows.

Essential Travel Tips

Get the Swiss Travel Pass

Provides unlimited rides on trains, buses, and lake ferries throughout Switzerland, plus 500+ free museum entries and 50% mountain cable car discounts.

Almost Entirely Cashless

Credit cards and mobile payments are accepted everywhere, even at small remote mountain huts.

Dress in Versatile Layers

Weather changes rapidly in high altitudes. Even in July, summit peaks like Jungfraujoch hover below freezing; pack a windproof fleece and sunglasses.
