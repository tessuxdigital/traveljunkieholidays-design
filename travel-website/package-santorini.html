Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in Santorini & Cliffside Cave Suite Check-In

Imerovigli, Santorini, Greece
Private VIP Mercedes Chauffeur Transfer Caldera Welcome Wine & Greek Mezze Grace Hotel Caldera Suite (5-Star Boutique)

Land at Thira Santorini Airport or port where your private driver greets you. Ascend the volcanic caldera ridgeline to picturesque Imerovigli, known as the balcony to the Aegean. Check into your cave suite featuring minimalist cycladic design and a heated terrace hot tub overlooking the shimmering sea.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Santorini Airport Imerovigli Caldera Ridge Skaros Rock Viewpoint Private Terrace Sunset
02
Day 2

Historic Oia Walking Tour & Iconic Blue Domes

Oia, Santorini
Private Chauffeur Breakfast, Seafood Lunch, Gourmet Dinner Grace Hotel Caldera Suite

Spend the morning strolling through the fairytale marble alleys of Oia. Capture photos of the iconic blue-domed churches, browse independent art galleries and bookshops, and descend the steps to Ammoudi Bay for grilled Aegean octopus. In the evening, watch the golden sun dip below the horizon from Oia Castle.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Oia Blue Domes Byzantine Castle Ruins Ammoudi Fishing Harbor Atlantis Books
03
Day 3

Caldera Luxury Catamaran Cruise & Volcanic Springs

Santorini Caldera & Nea Kameni
50ft Sailing Catamaran Breakfast, Barbecue Lunch Aboard, Sunset Cocktails Grace Hotel Caldera Suite

Embark on a luxury catamaran cruise into the sunken volcanic caldera. Swim in the thermal sulfur springs of Nea Kameni, snorkel off the vibrant cliffs of Red Beach and White Beach, and savor an authentic Greek BBQ lunch prepared freshly on board by the skipper.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Nea Kameni Volcanic Springs Red Beach Cliffs White Beach Cove Indian Rock & Aspronisi
04
Day 4

Volcanic Vineyards & Prehistoric Akrotiri

Pyrgos & Akrotiri, Santorini
Private Air-Conditioned Van Breakfast, Vineyard Wine Pairing Lunch Grace Hotel Caldera Suite

Discover the ancient agricultural heart of Santorini. Visit the Bronze Age ruins of Akrotiri, preserved beneath volcanic ash for 3,500 years. Continue to historic hillside village Pyrgos for guided cellar tours and structured tastings of crisp mineral Assyrtiko and sweet amber Vinsanto wines.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Akrotiri Archaeological Site Pyrgos Medieval Village Santo Wines Clifftop Estate Venetsanos Winery
05
Day 5

Scenic Caldera Rim Hike from Imerovigli to Fira

Fira & Firostefani, Santorini
Guided Walking Tour & Chauffeur Return Breakfast, Traditional Taverna Dinner Grace Hotel Caldera Suite

Hike the world-famous cliff trail tracing the rim from Imerovigli past Firostefani into vibrant Fira. Admire panoramic views of the entire crescent island. Browse the gold jewelry and linen boutiques of Fira, visit the Museum of Prehistoric Thira, and enjoy sunset cocktails on a clifftop terrace.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Caldera Rim Pathway Three Bells of Fira Museum of Prehistoric Thira Fira Old Harbor Cable Car
06
Day 6

Black Sand Beaches of Perissa & Private Candlelight Dinner

Perissa & Megalochori, Santorini
Private Vehicle Breakfast, Beach Club Lunch, 5-Course Farewell Dinner Grace Hotel Caldera Suite

Spend a relaxing day on the southeast coast where black volcanic sand meets crystal clear waters beneath Mount Mesa Vouno. Relax on plush sunbeds at a boutique beach club, wander the quiet bell towers of Megalochori, and cap your romantic getaway with a private candlelit degustation dinner on your caldera terrace.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Perissa Black Sand Beach Megalochori Traditional Square Mesa Vouno Mountain Private Terrace Degustation
07
Day 7

Farewell Santorini & Airport Transfer

Santorini Airport
Private Mercedes Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Hotel Check-out

Savor a final morning coffee gazing over the endless blue expanse of the Aegean Sea. Complete check-out before your private driver transfers you to Santorini Thira Airport or Athinios Ferry Port for your onward journey.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Morning Caldera Coffee Grace Hotel Check-out Santorini Airport Transfer
