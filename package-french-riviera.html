Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Helicopter Transfer from Nice to Monaco Hôtel de Paris

Nice to Monte-Carlo, Monaco
Monacair 7-Minute Helicopter Transfer & Limousine Welcome Champagne & Caviar Reception Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo (Sea View Suite)

Land at Nice Côte d’Azur Airport and step directly into a private helicopter for a breathtaking 7-minute flight tracing the dramatic cliffs of the French Riviera into Monaco Heliport. Check in to the legendary Hôtel de Paris on Casino Square and sip champagne on your terrace.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Nice Côte d’Azur Airport Monaco Heliport Flight Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo Casino de Monte-Carlo Square
02
Day 2

Private Yacht Charter: Cap Ferrat & Villefranche Bay

Monaco & Cap Ferrat
80ft Sunseeker Private Yacht Breakfast, Lobster & Champagne Yacht Lunch, Gourmet Dinner Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo

Board an 80ft luxury yacht in Port Hercule. Cruise past the clifftop villas of Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, dropping anchor in the crystal-clear turquoise waters of Villefranche Bay for swimming, snorkeling, and seabob riding with a private chef lunch prepared on board.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Port Hercule Yacht Marina Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat Peninsula Villefranche-sur-Mer Bay Plage Mala Clifftop Cove
03
Day 3

Prince’s Palace of Monaco & 3-Star Michelin Dinner

Monaco-Ville & Monte-Carlo
Private Chauffeur Breakfast, Portside Lunch, Le Louis XV Alain Ducasse Dinner Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo

Explore the Rock of Monaco, visiting the State Apartments of the Prince’s Palace and the Oceanographic Museum founded by Prince Albert I. In the evening, dress in black tie for dinner at Le Louis XV by Alain Ducasse, holder of 3 Michelin stars since 1990.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Prince’s Palace State Apartments Monaco Cathedral (Grace Kelly Tomb) Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo Spa Le Louis XV Restaurant
04
Day 4

Moyenne Corniche Drive, Eze & Transfer to Cannes

Eze & Cannes, France
Chauffeur-Driven Mercedes Maybach Breakfast, Clifftop Lunch at La Chèvre d’Or, Dinner Carlton Cannes, a Regent Hotel (Croisette Suite)

Drive the dramatic Moyenne Corniche highway up to the medieval eagle’s nest village of Eze. Savor lunch on the panoramic terrace of Château de la Chèvre d’Or before continuing to Cannes. Check in at the Belle Époque landmark Carlton Cannes on Boulevard de la Croisette.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Château de la Chèvre d’Or Jardin Exotique d’Eze Carlton Cannes Hotel Boulevard de la Croisette
05
Day 5

Saint-Tropez Yacht Day Trip & Pampelonne Beach Club

Cannes to Saint-Tropez
High-Speed Luxury Yacht Charter Breakfast, Club 55 Beach Lunch, Cannes Seafood Dinner Carlton Cannes

Cruise across the Gulf of Saint-Tropez past the red porphyry rock cliffs of the Massif de l’Esterel. Dock at Pampelonne Beach for a reserved VIP daybed and lunch at the world-famous Club 55, followed by a stroll through the cobblestone alleys of the old fishing village.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Massif de l’Esterel Red Cliffs Pampelonne Beach Club 55 VIP Lounge Saint-Tropez Harbor & Citadel
06
Day 6

Bespoke Grasse Perfume Creation & Lérins Islands Cruise

Grasse & Île Sainte-Marguerite
Private Chauffeur & Speedboat Tender Breakfast, Island Monastery Lunch, Farewell Gala Dinner Carlton Cannes

Travel inland to Grasse, the world capital of perfume. In a private laboratory, compose your own personalized eau de parfum with an esteemed master nose. In the afternoon, boat to the tranquil pine-scented Lérins Islands to explore the medieval fortress of the Man in the Iron Mask.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Private Grasse Perfume Atelier Île Sainte-Marguerite Fortress Île Saint-Honorat Abbey Vineyard Cannes Old Port Sunset
07
Day 7

Morning Croisette Stroll & Chauffeur to Nice Airport

Cannes to Nice Airport
Private Executive Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Savor espresso on the Carlton Cannes terrace looking out over the Mediterranean. Complete check-out before your private chauffeur transfers you to Nice Côte d’Azur Airport for your return flight home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Carlton Beach Club Cannes Old Port Nice Côte d’Azur Airport (NCE)
