Paris & The French Riviera

From the gilded salons of Versailles and twilight cruises along the Seine to the azure Mediterranean coastlines of Cannes and Nice.

Destination Overview

France embodies the world’s gold standard for art, gastronomy, and romance. In Paris, every Haussmann boulevard tells a story of artistic rebellion, royal splendor, and philosophical triumph. From the summit of the Eiffel Tower across Montmartre to the iconic Mona Lisa at the Louvre, the city enchants the soul.

A swift ride aboard the high-speed TGV rail brings you to the glamorous Côte d’Azur. Here, sun-drenched palm trees border turquoise Mediterranean shores, superyachts drop anchor in Cannes and Monaco, and hillside villages like Èze offer clifftop panoramas over golden waters.

Gustave Eiffel’s wrought-iron masterpiece rising 330 meters above the Champ de Mars with spectacular panoramic views.

The Louvre Museum

The world’s largest art museum holding over 35,000 treasures, including the Mona Lisa, Venus de Milo, and Winged Victory.

Skip-the-Line Priority Pass

Palace of Versailles

The Sun King Louis XIV’s monumental royal palace, featuring the 357-mirror Hall of Mirrors and grand fountain gardens.

Nice’s iconic 7km palm-lined Mediterranean boulevard overlooking the sparkling Bay of Angels and Belle Époque villas.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (April – June) · Blossom Season Prime Season

Pleasant 20°C temperatures, blooming Tuileries and Luxembourg gardens, outdoor cafe terraces bustling, and longer daylight.

Summer (July – August) · Riviera High Season Beach Paradise

Sun-soaked Mediterranean waters, film festivals, coastal glamour, and warm evening outdoor dining under the stars.

Autumn (September – November) · Wine Harvest Fewer Crowds

Grape harvests in Champagne and Bordeaux, pleasant walking weather, and world-class exhibitions without peak queues.

Essential Travel Tips for France

Paris Museum Pass

Grants skip-the-ticket-line entry into over 50 top museums and monuments including the Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, and Versailles.

Always Greet with "Bonjour"

Saying a polite "Bonjour" when entering any shop or cafe is the fundamental social courtesy in France.

Efficient Metro & TGV

The Paris Metro has a station every 500 meters. The high-speed TGV train connects Paris to Nice in just 5 hours and 30 minutes.
