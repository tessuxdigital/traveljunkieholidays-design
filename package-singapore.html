Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Changi Jewel Rain Vortex & Gardens by the Bay Light Show

Marina Bay, Singapore
Private Executive Chauffeur Transfer Welcome Singapore Sling & Jumbo Seafood Chili Crab Dinner Marina Bay Sands / The Fullerton Hotel Singapore (5-Star)

Touch down at award-winning Singapore Changi Airport and view the world’s tallest indoor waterfall, the Jewel Rain Vortex. Transfer to your luxury hotel overlooking Marina Bay. In the evening, visit Gardens by the Bay to walk the skyway between colossal solar Supertrees and watch the "Garden Rhapsody" light and music spectacle.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Jewel Changi Rain Vortex Marina Bay Waterfront Gardens by the Bay Supertree Grove Garden Rhapsody Light Show
02
Day 2

Cloud Forest, Chinatown Heritage & Singapore River Cruise

Marina Bay & Historic Quarters
Private Van & Electric River Boat Breakfast, Chinatown Hawker Lunch, Marina Bay Sands Dinner Marina Bay Sands / The Fullerton Hotel

Step inside the misty climate-controlled glass conservatory of the Cloud Forest to see a 35-meter indoor mountain waterfall surrounded by rare orchids. Explore Chinatown’s Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, the colorful shophouses of Kampong Glam, and board an electric bumboat cruising along the Singapore River to Clarke Quay.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Cloud Forest & Flower Dome Buddha Tooth Relic Temple Kampong Glam Sultan Mosque Historic Singapore River Cruise
03
Day 3

Sentosa Island Cable Car, S.E.A. Aquarium & Siloso Sunset

Sentosa Island, Singapore
Mount Faber Aerial Cable Car Breakfast, Beach Club Lunch, Coastal Sunset Dinner Marina Bay Sands / The Fullerton Hotel

Soar on the Singapore Cable Car across Keppel Harbour to Sentosa Island. Explore the monumental S.E.A. Aquarium, home to over 100,000 marine animals including hammerhead sharks and giant manta rays. Spend a relaxing afternoon on the golden sands of Siloso Beach.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Mount Faber Cable Car Station S.E.A. Aquarium Giant Viewing Panel Siloso Beach Palm Promenade Wings of Time Multi-Sensory Night Show
04
Day 4

Merlion Park, Orchard Road Shopping & Changi Departure

Civic District to Changi Airport
Private Executive Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Photograph the mythical water-spouting Merlion statue framed by the Marina skyline. Take a shopping stroll down the leafy boulevards of Orchard Road before checking out. Your private chauffeur transfers you to Changi Airport for your return flight.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Merlion Park Lookout Civic District & Old Parliament Orchard Road Boutiques Changi Airport Departure Terminal
