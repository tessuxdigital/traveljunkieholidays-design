Rolls-Royce Airport Pickup & Burj Al Arab Check-In

Dubai, UAE

Downtown Dubai & Dubai Marina
Private Mercedes Maybach Chauffeur & 75ft Mega-Yacht Breakfast, Atmosphere Level 122 Lunch, Yacht Dinner Burj Al Arab Jumeirah

Bypass all lines to the highest outdoor observation deck on earth at Burj Khalifa Level 148 Sky Lounge. Dine 442 meters up at At.mosphere restaurant. In the afternoon, board an exclusive 75ft private yacht cruising along the Dubai Marina, Ain Dubai wheel, and around the crescent of Palm Jumeirah.

Day 3

Heritage Desert Conservation Safari & Royal Falconry

Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve
Museum-Condition 1950s Vintage Land Rover Breakfast, Traditional Bedouin Feast with Shisha & Oud Al Maha, A Luxury Collection Desert Resort & Spa

Journey into the untouched dunes of the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve. Spot rare Arabian oryx and gazelles. Experience a royal falconry demonstration with the Shaheen falcons of the Royal Falconers. Conclude with a traditional Emirati dinner under starry desert skies at an exclusive private desert camp.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve Royal Falconry Demonstration Private Bedouin Desert Camp Dune Stargazing with Astronomer
04
Day 4

Abu Dhabi Day Tour: Sheikh Zayed Mosque & Louvre

Abu Dhabi, UAE
Private Chauffeur Sedan Breakfast, Emirates Palace 24k Gold Cappuccino Lunch, Dinner Burj Al Arab Jumeirah

Travel along the modern coastal highway to the capital of Abu Dhabi. Tour the monumental Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque with its 82 white marble domes and world’s largest hand-knotted Persian carpet. Visit the floating dome of the Louvre Abu Dhabi and enjoy high tea at Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Louvre Abu Dhabi Museum Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental Qasr Al Watan Presidential Palace
05
Day 5

Palm Jumeirah Helicopter Flight & Talise Spa Luxury

Palm Jumeirah, Dubai
Private VIP Helicopter & Chauffeur Breakfast, Beach Club Lunch, Michelin-Star Pierchic Dinner Burj Al Arab Jumeirah

Take to the skies on a 25-minute helicopter flight over the World Islands, Atlantis The Palm, and Dubai Creek. Spend an afternoon of pampering at the Talise Spa perched 150 meters above the Persian Gulf. In the evening, walk out over the illuminated private pier to Pierchic for seafood.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Atlantis Helipad The World Islands Aerial View Talise Spa Elevated Pools Pierchic Overwater Restaurant

Day 6

Leisurely Breakfast & Chauffeur Transfer to DXB

Dubai International Airport
Private Rolls-Royce Chauffeur Transfer Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy a lavish breakfast on your private suite terrace overlooking the azure gulf. Complete check-out before your Rolls-Royce chauffeur whisks you back to Dubai International Airport (DXB) VIP terminal for your flight departure.

Suite Terrace Breakfast Burj Al Arab Check-out DXB VIP Terminal Departure
