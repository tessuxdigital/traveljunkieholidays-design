Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in New Delhi & Welcome to India

New Delhi, India
Private Air-Conditioned Chauffeur Transfer Traditional Welcome Garland & Mughlai Dinner The Imperial New Delhi / The Leela Palace New Delhi (5-Star)

Arrive at Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) in New Delhi. Your private chauffeur meets you at the exit gate with traditional marigold garlands and escorts you to your luxury heritage palace hotel. Relax and recover from your flight with fine Mughlai cuisine.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Indira Gandhi International Airport Connaught Place The Imperial Hotel Heritage Corridors India Gate Boulevard
02
Day 2

Old Delhi Rickshaw Safari & New Delhi Monuments

Delhi
Private Chauffeur Sedan & Cycle Rickshaw Breakfast, Karim’s Historic Lunch, Dinner The Imperial New Delhi

Board a cycle rickshaw winding through the vibrant spice markets of Chandni Chowk and Khari Baoli. Tour the monumental Jama Masjid mosque. In the afternoon, explore New Delhi’s grand colonial vistas: the India Gate war memorial, President’s House, UNESCO Humayun’s Tomb, and the 73-meter Qutub Minar.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Jama Masjid Great Mosque Chandni Chowk & Khari Baoli Spice Market Humayun’s Garden Tomb Qutub Minar Tower & Iron Pillar
03
Day 3

Express Highway to Agra & Sunset Taj Mahal from Mehtab Bagh

Delhi to Agra, Uttar Pradesh
Yamuna Expressway Private Chauffeur Drive (3.5 hours) Breakfast, Mughlai Curry Lunch, Dinner The Oberoi Amarvilas Agra / ITC Mughal Agra (5-Star)

Drive via the Yamuna Expressway to Agra, the capital of the Mughal Empire at its peak. Check into your hotel where all rooms overlook the Taj Mahal. In the late afternoon, visit the gardens of Mehtab Bagh across the Yamuna River to watch the setting sun reflect against the white marble dome.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Yamuna Expressway Mehtab Bagh (Moonlight Garden) Yamuna Riverbank Sunset View Agra Marble Inlay Artisan Workshop
04
Day 4

Sunrise at the Taj Mahal & Historic Agra Fort

Agra
Private Vehicle & Battery-Operated Golf Cart Breakfast, Royal Agra Lunch, Dinner The Oberoi Amarvilas Agra

Pass through the royal gates of the Taj Mahal at dawn. Watch the ivory-white marble glow in soft pink and golden morning light. Walk through the interior cenotaph chambers inlaid with semi-precious lapis lazuli and jasper. In the afternoon, explore the red-sandstone fortress of Agra Fort.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Taj Mahal Sunrise Visit Mughal Charbagh Water Gardens Agra Fort (Red Fort) Jahangiri Mahal & Diwan-i-Khas
05
Day 5

Fatehpur Sikri Ghost City & Drive to the Pink City of Jaipur

Agra to Jaipur, Rajasthan
Private Air-Conditioned Chauffeur Drive (4.5 hours) Breakfast, Heritage Haveli Lunch, Rajasthani Thali Dinner Rambagh Palace Jaipur / Fairmont Jaipur (5-Star)

En route to Rajasthan, stop at the preserved red-sandstone ghost city of Fatehpur Sikri, built by Emperor Akbar and abandoned due to water scarcity. Cross into the land of Rajput kings to Jaipur, the "Pink City." Check in at the royal Rambagh Palace, former residence of the Maharaja of Jaipur.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Fatehpur Sikri Imperial Complex Buland Darwaza (Gate of Magnificence) Abhaneri Stepwell (Chand Baori) Jaipur Pink City Gates
06
Day 6

Hilltop Amber Fort Ascent & Jal Mahal Water Palace

Jaipur, Rajasthan
Jeep Ascent & Private Chauffeur Breakfast, Royal Jaipur Lunch, Palace Dinner Rambagh Palace Jaipur

Ascend to the hilltop Amber Fort overlooking Maota Lake by decorated 4x4 open jeep. Walk through the dazzling Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace) where walls are covered in thousands of convex Belgian mirrors. Stop at the water palace of Jal Mahal floating in Man Sagar Lake.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Amber Fort & Fort Ramparts Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace) Jal Mahal Floating Palace View Anokhi Hand-Block Printing Museum
07
Day 7

City Palace, Jantar Mantar Observatory & Hawa Mahal

Jaipur
Private Vehicle & Walking Breakfast, Heritage Cafe Lunch, Royal Farewell Feast Rambagh Palace Jaipur

Explore the City Palace of Jaipur, still home to the titular Maharaja family, with its Peacock Gate and armory collections. Visit the UNESCO Jantar Mantar astronomical observatory containing the world’s largest stone sundial. Photograph the iconic 953-window façade of Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds).

Featured Spots & Activities:
Jaipur City Palace & Museums Pritam Niwas Chowk (Peacock Gate) Jantar Mantar UNESCO Observatory Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)
08
Day 8

Scenic Drive Back to Delhi & Airport Departure

Jaipur to Delhi Airport
Private Highway Chauffeur Transfer (4.5 hours) Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast on the manicured palace lawns. Complete check-out before your private chauffeur transfers you via the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway directly to Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) in Delhi for your return flight home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Palace Courtyard Morning Delhi-Jaipur Expressway Delhi International Airport (DEL)
