Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Nairobi Arrival & Safari Drive to Amboseli

Nairobi to Amboseli National Park
Custom 4x4 Safari Land Cruiser with Pop-Up Roof Welcome Safari Buffet Lunch & Lodge Dinner Ol Tukai Lodge Amboseli / Serena Safari Lodge

Meet your private safari guide at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport in Nairobi. Drive south through Maasai cattle lands into Amboseli National Park. Embark on an afternoon game drive as the snow-covered volcanic dome of Mount Kilimanjaro appears on the horizon.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Nairobi JKIA Airport Great North Safari Highway Amboseli National Park Entrance Observation Hill Sunset Lookout
02
Day 2

Amboseli Big Tuskers & Enkongo Swamp Game Drives

Amboseli National Park
4x4 Safari Land Cruiser Breakfast, Bush Picnic Lunch, Campfire Dinner Ol Tukai Lodge Amboseli

Rise at dawn for a morning game drive when Mount Kilimanjaro is clearest. Watch massive herds of elephants wading through the emerald marshes of Enkongo Narok swamp, flanked by hippos, zebras, giraffes, and cheetahs hunting on the open savannah.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Enkongo Narok Swamps Mount Kilimanjaro Backdrop Lake Amboseli Dried Salt Flats Acacia Tortilis Woodlands
03
Day 3

Great Rift Valley & Lake Naivasha Boat Safari

Amboseli to Lake Naivasha
4x4 Land Cruiser & Motorized Lake Boat Breakfast, Rift Valley Lodge Lunch, Dinner Lake Naivasha Sopa Resort

Drive past the dramatic escarpment of the Great Rift Valley down to freshwater Lake Naivasha. Board an open boat safari skimming past pods of bathing hippos and African fish eagles diving for prey. Disembark for a walking safari among wild zebras and giraffes on Crescent Island.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Great Rift Valley Viewpoint Lake Naivasha Shoreline Crescent Island Game Sanctuary Hell’s Gate National Park Gorge
04
Day 4

Journey to the Maasai Mara National Reserve

Lake Naivasha to Maasai Mara
4x4 Safari Land Cruiser Breakfast, Mara Bush Lunch, Luxury Tented Camp Dinner Governor’s Camp / Mara Serena Safari Lodge

Ascend the Mau Escarpment and cross the fertile Narok wheat plains into the legendary Maasai Mara National Reserve. Check in to your luxury canvas tented suite perched over the Mara River where hippos wallow below. Head out on your first late-afternoon Mara predator game drive.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Narok Town Sekenani Gate Maasai Mara Mara River Basin Sunset Predator Tracking
05
Day 5

Full Day Mara River Crossing & Migration Tracking

Maasai Mara Savanna
4x4 Safari Land Cruiser Breakfast, Picnic Lunch Under Acacia Tree, Boma Dinner Governor’s Camp Maasai Mara

Spend an entire day tracking the wildlife spectacle of the African continent. Follow million-strong herds of wildebeests and zebras along the Mara River crossings. Watch pride of lions, solitary leopards, and coalitions of cheetahs stalking herds across the golden red-oat grass.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Mara River Crossing Points Paradise Plains Rhino Ridge Serengeti-Mara International Border Marker
06
Day 6

Sunrise Hot Air Balloon Safari & Maasai Village Visit

Maasai Mara
Hot Air Balloon & 4x4 Land Cruiser Bush Champagne Breakfast, Camp Lunch, Farewell Feast Governor’s Camp Maasai Mara

Before dawn, float soundlessly over the acacia plains in a hot air balloon as herds gallop below. Touch down for a white-linen champagne breakfast cooked in the wild bush. In the afternoon, visit an authentic Maasai manyatta village to participate in traditional ceremonies and fire-making.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sunrise Balloon Launch Site Acacia Tree Champagne Breakfast Maasai Manyatta Boma Village Twilight Campfire Stargazing
07
Day 7

Morning Bush Safari & Flight to Nairobi Departure

Maasai Mara to Nairobi
Bush Airstrip Flight (Cessna Caravan) & Chauffeur Breakfast Included Check-out

Enjoy an early morning dawn game drive capturing the golden sunrise light. Fly from the bush airstrip directly back to Wilson Airport in Nairobi, where your private driver transfers you to Jomo Kenyatta Airport or the historic Carnivore restaurant for dinner before your flight home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Mara Bush Airstrip Wilson Airport Nairobi Giraffe Centre / Karen Blixen Museum Nairobi JKIA Airport
