Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Arrival in Paris & Seine River Sunset Cruise

Paris, France
Private Chauffeur Sedan Welcome Champagne & French Macarons, Bistro Dinner Hotel Plaza Athénée / Le Bristol Paris

Arrive at Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport and meet your private chauffeur for transfer to your luxury boutique hotel in the heart of Saint-Germain-des-Prés. As dusk falls, board a glass-canopied Seine River cruise gliding beneath illuminated historic bridges past Notre-Dame and the twinkling Eiffel Tower.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Charles de Gaulle Airport Saint-Germain-des-Prés Pont Alexandre III Illuminated Seine River Cruise
02
Day 2

VIP Louvre Access & Eiffel Tower Summit

Paris
Private Van & Walking Breakfast, Café Lunch, Eiffel Tower Dinner Hotel Plaza Athénée Paris

Enjoy an exclusive early-entry guided tour of the Louvre Museum with an art historian. Stand before the Mona Lisa before the crowds arrive. Stroll through the Tuileries Garden and Place Vendôme. In the evening, ascend by VIP elevator to the summit of the Eiffel Tower, followed by reservations at the Michelin-starred Madame Brasserie.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Louvre Museum VIP Entrance Tuileries Gardens Eiffel Tower Summit Champs-Élysées
03
Day 3

Royal Palace of Versailles & Hall of Mirrors

Versailles, France
Private Executive Chauffeur Breakfast, Royal Estate Lunch Hotel Plaza Athénée Paris

Journey 40 minutes west of Paris to the Sun King’s opulent Château de Versailles. Walk through the glittering Hall of Mirrors, the King and Queen State Apartments, and explore the vast manicured geometric fountains and gardens. Rent a private electric carriage or golf buggy to visit Marie Antoinette’s rustic Petit Trianon.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Château de Versailles Hall of Mirrors Grand Canal & Fountains Queen’s Hamlet (Hameau)
04
Day 4

Bohemian Montmartre & Latin Quarter Food Walk

Montmartre, Paris
Walking Tour & Funicular Breakfast, Artisan Bakery & Fromagerie Tasting Hotel Plaza Athénée Paris

Climb the winding cobblestone steps of Montmartre up to the white-domed Sacré-Cœur Basilica overlooking the city. Meet portrait artists at Place du Tertre and taste artisanal croissants and aged cheeses on a culinary stroll through Paris historic market streets.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sacré-Cœur Basilica Place du Tertre Moulin Rouge Exterior Rue Cler Gourmet Market
05
Day 5

First-Class TGV to Nice & Promenade des Anglais

Paris to Nice, French Riviera
First-Class TGV High-Speed Train & Mercedes Transfer Breakfast, Gourmet Train Bistro, Niçoise Seafood Dinner Hotel Negresco Nice (5-Star Landmark)

Board the high-speed TGV train from Gare de Lyon, traveling at 300 km/h through Burgundy vineyards and Provence lavender valleys down to the blue Mediterranean. Check in at the legendary Hotel Negresco on the Promenade des Anglais in Nice and explore the colorful flower market at Cours Saleya.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Paris Gare de Lyon High-Speed TGV Journey Nice Promenade des Anglais Old Town (Vieux Nice) & Cours Saleya
06
Day 6

Monaco Glamour, Monte Carlo & Medieval Eze

Monaco & Eze Village
Private Riviera Panoramic Chauffeur Breakfast, Clifftop Lunch, Monte Carlo Dinner Hotel Negresco Nice

Drive the dramatic Moyenne Corniche cliffside highway to the eagle’s nest village of Eze. Visit the Fragonard perfume factory and the Exotic Botanical Garden with sweeping views of Cap Ferrat. Cross into the Principality of Monaco to view the Prince’s Palace, yacht harbor, and the lavish Monte Carlo Casino.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Medieval Village of Eze Fragonard Perfumery Principality of Monaco Monte Carlo Casino Square
07
Day 7

Cap d’Antibes Catamaran Sail & Cannes Boulevard

Antibes & Cannes, France
Luxury Sailing Catamaran & Private Transfer Breakfast, Seaside Seafood Lunch, Farewell Dinner Hotel Negresco Nice

Sail along the billionaire peninsula of Cap d’Antibes dropping anchor in crystal-clear turquoise coves for swimming and paddleboarding. In the afternoon, visit Cannes for a stroll down Boulevard de la Croisette, past palatial beach clubs and the Palais des Festivals.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Cap d’Antibes Coastal Waters Billionaires Bay Cove Cannes Boulevard de la Croisette Picasso Museum Antibes
08
Day 8

Morning Mediterranean Stroll & Departure

Nice, France
Private Airport Chauffeur Breakfast Included Check-out

Take one last morning stroll along Castle Hill overlooking the Baie des Anges. Enjoy café au lait and fresh croissants before checking out. Your private chauffeur transfers you to Nice Côte d’Azur Airport for your return flight.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Castle Hill (Colline du Château) Hotel Negresco Check-out Nice Côte d’Azur Airport
