Discover the rich heritage and natural wonders of Vietnam on this exclusive 5-day journey. From the tree-lined boulevards and historic pagodas of Hanoi to the emerald karst waters of Halong Bay, and onward to the lantern-lit streets of Hoi An and the high-altitude Golden Bridge of Ba Na Hills.

Hand-curated moments designed to give you memories of a lifetime.

Halong Bay Day Cruise

Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

ARRIVAL IN HANOI

Hanoi, Vietnam
Private transfer Welcome Dinner Included Apricot Hotel Hanoi (5-Star)

Hanoi arrival + Hanoi Half Day City Tour. Your private transfer meets you at Noi Bai International Airport and whisks you directly to your luxury hotel. In the afternoon, set out with your dedicated private guide for an immersive exploration of the millennium-old capital, taking in sacred pagodas, historic political centers, and bustling merchant quarters.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Tran Quoc Pagoda Train Street Hoan Kiem Lake
02
Day 2

HALONG BAY

Halong Bay, Quang Ninh, Vietnam
Private Coach & Day Cruise Boat Lunch & Dinner Included Apricot Hotel Hanoi (5-Star)

Full-day Halong Bay day cruise. Travel via scenic expressway to the Gulf of Tonkin to embark on your cruise ship. Glide effortlessly across jade-green waters through thousands of towering limestone karsts. Explore magnificent dripstone caves, climb Ti Top Island for 360-degree panoramic bay vistas, and savor both a gourmet seafood lunch and an evening dinner aboard.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Full-day Halong Bay day cruise Sung Sot Cave Ti Top Island Lookout Lunch and Dinner Aboard
03
Day 3

DA NANG & HOI AN

Da Nang & Hoi An, Vietnam
Fly to Da Nang & Private Vehicle Breakfast & Traditional Hoi An Dinner Anantara Hoi An Riverside Resort

Fly to Da Nang. Transfer to the airport for your flight to coastal central Vietnam. Upon arrival, proceed to the lush Cam Thanh Coconut Village for an authentic Basket Boat Ride. In the afternoon, delve into UNESCO-listed Hoi An Ancient Town with its yellow merchant shophouses. In the evening, step onto a wooden sampan for a tranquil Release Flower Lantern Boat Ride.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Fly to Da Nang Coconut Village Basket Boat Ride Hoi An Ancient Town Release Flower Lantern Boat Ride
04
Day 4

DA NANG – BA NA HILLS

Ba Na Hills, Da Nang, Vietnam
Cable Car & Private Coach Breakfast & Summit Buffet Lunch Anantara Hoi An Riverside Resort

Ba Na Hills, Golden Bridge, Cable Car. Ride the Guinness World Record Cable Car scaling above lush misty rainforest waterfalls. Step out onto the Golden Bridge, suspended 1,414 meters above sea level and held aloft by giant mossy stone hands. Explore the French Village, Linh Ung Pagoda, and alpine gardens.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Ba Na Hills Golden Bridge Cable Car French Village & Fantasy Park
05
Day 5

DEPARTURE

Da Nang, Vietnam
Private Airport Transfer Breakfast Included Hotel Check-out

Hotel check-out. Transfer to the airport for your return flight. Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and morning by the pool or river. Complete check-out procedures, then your private transfer delivers you comfortably to Da Nang International Airport for your journey onward.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Hotel check-out Transfer to the airport for your return flight Da Nang International Airport
