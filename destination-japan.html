Tokyo & Ancient Kyoto

A captivating juxtaposition of neon-lit cybernetic skylines, tranquil moss gardens, thousands of vermilion torii gates, and culinary mastery.

Capital: Tokyo Currency: Japanese Yen (JPY ¥) Language: Japanese (English in transit) Ideal Stay: 10–14 Days Spring Temp: 19°C (66°F)

Destination Overview

Japan presents an extraordinary harmony where centuries-old traditions flourish alongside lightning-speed technological marvels. In Tokyo, sensory immersion awaits in the neon canyons of Shinjuku, the electronic labyrinth of Akihabara, and peaceful precincts like Meiji Jingu shrine.

Boarding the Shinkansen bullet train at 320 km/h brings you to Kyoto, the imperial cultural heart of Japan for over a millennium. Here, geishas glide along lantern-lit Pontocho alleys, 10,000 torii gates snake through sacred cedar forests at Fushimi Inari, and peaceful rock gardens invite profound contemplation.

Famous Tourist Attractions

Fushimi Inari Taisha

Walk through thousands of vibrant orange torii gates dedicated to Inari, the Shinto god of rice and prosperity.

Open 24 Hours · Free

Mount Fuji & Lake Kawaguchiko

Japan's sacred volcano rising 3,776 meters above tranquil mirror lakes, framed by spring cherry blossoms and autumn maples.

UNESCO World Heritage

Senso-ji Temple & Asakusa

Tokyo’s oldest Buddhist temple founded in 645 AD. Enter through the iconic Kaminarimon thunder gate and Nakamise street.

Incense Purification

Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)

A Zen Buddhist temple whose top two floors are completely covered in pure gold leaf, reflecting serenely across Kyoko-chi pond.

Historic Kyoto Wonder

Popular Activities

Zen Tea Ceremony

Participate in the mindful ritual of Chado (The Way of Tea) guided by a kimono-clad tea master in a historic Daitoku-ji sub-temple.

Shinkansen Bullet Train

Glide from Tokyo to Kyoto in just 2 hours and 15 minutes, savoring an authentic Ekiben bento box with Mt. Fuji summit views.

Hakone Onsen Springs

Soak in natural mineral-rich geothermal hot spring waters overlooking cedar forests and snow-dusted volcano ridges.

Japanese Gastronomy & Culinary Arts

Edomae Omakase Sushi

Piece-by-piece fresh otoro tuna, sea urchin, and line-caught snapper brushed with aged nikiri soy by Michelin-starred sushi masters.

Tonkotsu & Shoyu Ramen

Rich 16-hour simmered pork bone broth with springy noodles, melt-in-your-mouth chashu pork, and seasoned soft-boiled ramen eggs.

Kyoto Traditional Kaiseki

The pinnacle of Japanese haute cuisine: a multi-course banquet of seasonal art reflecting Buddhist aesthetics and micro-seasons.

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March – May) · Cherry Blossom Season Sakura Bloom

Parks and riverbanks across Tokyo and Kyoto transform into seas of pink blossoms (Sakura). Enjoy Hanami picnic celebrations and perfect 18°C weather.

Autumn (October – November) · Koyo Foliage Brilliant Colors

Temples are illuminated at night to showcase fiery red and gold Japanese maple leaves. Crisp, clear skies ideal for Mt. Fuji photography.

Winter (December – February) · Clear Skies & Hot Springs Crisp & Peaceful

Minimal rain, clear visibility of Mount Fuji, quiet temples with dusted snow, and heavenly outdoor hot spring onsen soaks.

Essential Travel Tips for Japan

Suica / Pasmo IC Card

Tap to pay for subways, local buses, vending machines, and convenience stores nationwide. Add it directly to your Apple or Google Wallet.

No Tipping Culture

Tipping is not customary and can even be considered impolite. Exceptional service is built into every encounter as part of 'Omotenashi' hospitality.

Easy Slip-On Shoes

You will remove your shoes when entering traditional ryokans, temple halls, and tatami-mat restaurants. Wear comfortable, clean socks!
