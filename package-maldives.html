Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Seaplane Arrival & Overwater Villa Check-In

South Ari Atoll, Maldives
Trans Maldivian Airways Seaplane Transfer Welcome Champagne & Canapés, 3-Course Dinner Soneva Jani / Overwater Pool Villa

Touch down at Velana International Airport in Malé where your resort island concierge welcomes you into the private seaplane lounge. Board your panoramic seaplane flight soaring above the iridescent atoll rings. Settle into your standalone overwater villa equipped with a private infinity plunge pool and glass floor view into the turquoise ocean.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Velana International Airport Scenic Seaplane Flight Private Overwater Villa Jetty Lagoon Sunset Welcome
02
Day 2

Lagoon Relaxation & Sunset Dolphin Yacht Sail

South Ari Atoll
Luxury Sailing Catamaran Floating Breakfast, Gourmet Lunch, Dinner Soneva Jani Overwater Pool Villa

Wake to breakfast served floating on trays in your private pool. Spend a sun-soaked morning swimming directly from your villa deck into the lagoon. In the late afternoon, step aboard an elegant 60ft wooden dhoni yacht for a sunset sail to witness acrobatic pods of wild spinner dolphins leaping against the golden horizon.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Floating Breakfast Experience Turquoise Lagoon Swimming Sunset Dolphin Yacht Safari Sandbank Stargazing
03
Day 3

House Reef Coral Safari & Manta Ray Encounter

South Ari Marine Reserve
Speedboat Tender Breakfast, Seafood Beach Barbecue, Dinner Soneva Jani Overwater Pool Villa

Join the resident marine biologist on an expedition to the protected coral walls of the South Ari Atoll. Snorkel alongside graceful manta rays, green sea turtles, and kaleidoscopic schools of butterflyfish and parrotfish. Return for an afternoon of paddleboarding and beachfront relaxation.

Featured Spots & Activities:
South Ari Marine Protected Reef Manta Ray Feeding Grounds Sea Turtle Lagoon Overwater Hammocks
04
Day 4

Holistic Overwater Spa & Candlelight Sandbank Dinner

South Ari Atoll
Resort Golf Buggy / Walking Breakfast, Light Pavilion Lunch, Private Beach Dinner Soneva Jani Overwater Pool Villa

Indulge in a 90-minute signature couples massage in an open-sided glass-bottomed spa pavilion listening to the gentle ocean surf. As twilight arrives, be whisked away to a secluded private sandbank where a table carved into the sand awaits, illuminated by torchlight for an unforgettable 5-course seafood dinner.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Overwater Ayurvedic Spa Private Sandbank Sanctuary Torchlight Candlelit Beach Open-Air Cinema Paradiso
05
Day 5

Kayaking, Coral Gardening & Underwater Dining

South Ari Atoll
Glass-Bottom Kayak Breakfast, Underwater Dining Lunch, Degustation Dinner Soneva Jani Overwater Pool Villa

Paddle clear glass-bottom kayaks across the sheltered atoll lagoon and participate in the resort coral regeneration initiative by planting your own coral fragment. For lunch, dine submerged five meters beneath the surface in the resort underwater restaurant surrounded by vibrant marine life.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Glass-Bottom Kayaking Coral Nursery Reef Submerged Underwater Restaurant Sunset Wine Cellar Degustation
06
Day 6

Morning Lagoon Dip & Seaplane Departure

South Ari Atoll to Malé
Scenic Return Seaplane Transfer Breakfast Included Resort Check-out

Enjoy a leisurely final dip in the Indian Ocean and a decadent tropical breakfast. Complete check-out before boarding your scenic return seaplane back to Malé Velana International Airport for your flight connection home.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Morning Lagoon Swim Resort Check-out Trans Maldivian Seaplane Terminal Malé International Airport
