Bali Coastal Villas & Nusa Penida

Complete Travel Schedule

Chronological Day-Wise Itinerary

100% Confirmed Schedule
01
Day 1

Denpasar Arrival & Private Seminyak Pool Villa Check-In

Denpasar to Seminyak, Bali
Private Air-Conditioned Chauffeur Transfer Tropical Welcome Drink & Balinese Seafood Dinner The Samaya Seminyak / W Bali Seminyak (Private Pool Villa)

Land at Ngurah Rai International Airport in Denpasar where your private Balinese host greets you with fragrant frangipani flower leis. Transfer to your beachfront private pool villa in fashionable Seminyak. Unwind in your sunken garden pool before a sunset dinner on the sand.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Denpasar Ngurah Rai Airport Seminyak Beachfront Private Villa Plunge Pool Petitenget Sunset Beach
02
Day 2

Tanah Lot Sea Temple & Jimbaran Sunset Seafood Feast

Tanah Lot & Jimbaran Bay
Private Vehicle Floating Breakfast, Jimbaran Beach Seafood Feast The Samaya Seminyak Private Pool Villa

Wake to an elaborate breakfast floating in your villa pool. In the afternoon, visit the dramatic 16th-century sea temple of Pura Tanah Lot perched on an offshore rock battered by crashing waves. Conclude at Jimbaran Bay for a candlelit feast of grilled snapper, lobster, and prawns right on the beach.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Tanah Lot Offshore Temple Echo Beach Canggu Jimbaran Bay Candlelit Beach Balinese Gamelan Sunset
03
Day 3

Speedboat to Nusa Penida: Kelingking & Broken Beach

Nusa Penida Island
Private Fast Boat & 4WD Island Vehicle Breakfast, Clifftop Lunch, Beach Club Dinner Maua Nusa Penida Luxury Eco-Resort

Board a private speedboat crossing the Badung Strait to the dramatic island of Nusa Penida. Visit the jaw-dropping T-Rex shaped coastal promontory of Kelingking Secret Point. Marvel at the natural rock archway of Broken Beach and the natural infinity tide pool of Angel’s Billabong.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sanur Harbor Fast Boat Kelingking T-Rex Cliff Broken Beach (Pasih Uug) Angel’s Billabong Natural Pool
04
Day 4

Manta Bay Snorkeling Safari & Crystal Bay

Nusa Penida & Manta Point
Private Snorkeling Boat & Return Speedboat to Bali Breakfast, Island Beach Lunch, Villa Dinner The Samaya Seminyak Private Pool Villa

Set out on an open boat to Manta Point, where resident oceanic manta rays glide through cleaning stations. Snorkel alongside these gentle giants with wingspans up to four meters. Swim among coral gardens in Crystal Bay before catching the afternoon fast boat back to main Bali.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Manta Point Feeding Sanctuary Crystal Bay Coral Gardens Gamat Bay Snorkel Reef Seminyak Evening Nightlife
05
Day 5

Cultural Ubud: Monkey Forest & Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Ubud, Gianyar, Bali
Private Chauffeur Tour Breakfast, Organic Jungle Farm Lunch, Ubud Bistro Dinner Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan / Mandapa

Travel into the spiritual heartland of Ubud. Walk the shaded jungle stone paths of the Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary. Climb the emerald stepped terraces of Tegallalang, try the famous jungle ravine swing, and browse woodcarvings and batik silks in Ubud market.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Sacred Monkey Forest Tegallalang Stepped Rice Terraces Bali Jungle Ravine Swing Ubud Royal Palace & Market
06
Day 6

Tirta Empul Water Blessing & Ayung River Rafting

Tampaksiring & Ayung River, Ubud
Private Vehicle & River Raft Breakfast, Riverside Lunch, Lotus Pavilion Dinner Four Seasons Resort Bali at Sayan

Participate in a centuries-old purification ritual at the sacred holy water springs of Tirta Empul Temple. Afterwards, embark on a fun, scenic white-water rafting trip down the Ayung River through lush tropical rainforests with carved stone riverbanks and hidden waterfalls.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Tirta Empul Holy Water Temple Ayung River Rainforest Rafting Pura Gunung Kawi Cliff Shrines Ayung River Valley Overlook
07
Day 7

Uluwatu Clifftop Temple & Dramatic Kecak Fire Dance

Uluwatu, Bukit Peninsula
Private Chauffeur Breakfast, Clifftop Beach Club Lunch, Farewell Banquet The Samaya Seminyak Private Pool Villa

Travel to the rugged limestone cliffs of the Bukit Peninsula. Tour Pura Luhur Uluwatu perched 70 meters above the crashing Indian Ocean. As the sun sets, take seats in the open amphitheater for the stirring Kecak Fire Dance telling the tale of the Ramayana.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Pura Luhur Uluwatu Sea Temple Kecak & Fire Dance Sunset Performance Padang Padang Surfing Beach Single Fin Clifftop Terrace
08
Day 8

Balinese Herbal Spa & Airport Departure

Seminyak to Denpasar Airport
Private Airport Chauffeur Breakfast Included Check-out

Conclude your Balinese journey with a 90-minute traditional Balinese boreh herbal massage and flower petal bath. Check out of your villa and take your private transfer to Ngurah Rai Airport for your return flight.

Featured Spots & Activities:
Villa Flower Petal Bath Seminyak Art Boutiques Denpasar Airport (DPS)
